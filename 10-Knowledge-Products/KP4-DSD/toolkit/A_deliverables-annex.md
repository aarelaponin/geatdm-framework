# The deliverables annex of the terms of reference for a supplier

The annex that names the twelve documents as deliverables in the contract, each with its blank instrument, the person who accepts it and the check it must pass. The application is named as generated from the accepted model, with hand edits excluded.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Who writes it** | The ministry's procurement officer, with the team that commissions the service |
| **What goes in** | The twelve documents, and the blank instrument of each |
| **Who accepts it** | The ministry's procurement officer reviews it against the procurement rules that apply, before it is issued |
| **Taught in** | 6.1 |

## How to use this blank instrument

Copy this page into your own document. Fill in the answer to every line. Do not leave a line empty: write "none" where nothing applies, and where the answer is not yet known write an open question with the name of its owner and a date. Do not guess an answer to close a question. When the document is finished, give it to the person who accepts it, together with the questions at the end of this page.

## Before you accept it

1. Does every one of the twelve documents appear, with a named person who accepts it?
2. Does every deliverable name the check it must pass?
3. Does the annex say that the application is generated from the accepted model and that hand edits are excluded?
4. Has the procurement officer reviewed it against the procurement rules that apply?
