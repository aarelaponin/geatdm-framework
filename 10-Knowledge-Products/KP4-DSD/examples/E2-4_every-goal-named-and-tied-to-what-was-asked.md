# E2.4 — The goals of PHEQA's and MoEYS's applications, checked in both directions

**What it shows.** The goals of the two applications as a list a manager can read, PHEQA's and MoEYS's, with the check in both directions: one entry of the register found served by no goal, and one goal found serving no entry, each made a question with an owner.

**Form of this example.** The check in both directions is the method's and names no country. The goals and entries are filled for Progressa.

## What a goal is

A goal is something one person wants to get done with the service, in one sitting, that leaves a result they can see. "Apply for a provisional licence" is a goal. "Maintain institution data" is not: nobody sits down to do it, and nobody can say when it is finished. A service is broken into the goals people have in it, and every goal is tied to the entries of the register (subtopic 2.2) it serves.

**Read down each column.** Every goal should have at least one ●. G-02, *record the application fee*, has none: no entry of the register asks for a fee. Either the regulations ask for one and the register missed it, or the goal records something nobody asked for.

**Read along each row.** Every entry should have at least one ●. R-04, *the register is open to the public*, has none: no goal lets a member of the public look up an institution.

## The two findings, each made a question

Neither finding is fixed by the analyst on the spot. Each becomes a question with an owner.

| Question | Owner | Answer needed by | What happens on each answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Is a fee for an application asked anywhere in Progressa's law or in PHEQA's own rules? If so, where? | The finance officer of PHEQA | 6 November 2026 | If yes: the source is added to the register as a new entry, and G-02 serves it. If no: G-02 is struck out. |
| Who looks up the public register, and what do they need to see? | The Registrar of PHEQA | 6 November 2026 | A goal is added, *look up an institution in the public register*, with a member of the public as the person who has it; or the entry is set aside, with her reason, to a later project |

In Progressa the finance officer found the fee in a schedule PHEQA issued under article 7 of the regulations, which nobody had given the analyst. It became entry R-09, and G-02 now serves it. The Registrar added the public look-up as goal G-06.

**What to look for before you accept the list of goals.** Ask for the table with goals across and entries down. Look for an empty column and an empty row. An empty column is something the supplier will build that nobody asked for. An empty row is something you asked for that nobody will build.
