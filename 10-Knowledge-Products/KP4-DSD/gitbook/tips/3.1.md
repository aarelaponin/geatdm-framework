## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa.

**[paste the entries the goal serves]:**

- **R-01.** "An application for a licence is made through PHEQA's self-service." Higher Education Regulations 2025, article 6.
- **R-09.** "The applicant pays the fee in PHEQA's schedule of fees when it applies." Article 7, and the finance officer of PHEQA at the requirements meeting of 22 September 2026.
- **R-10.** "The application carries the particulars the regulations list for the kind of institution." Article 6.
- **R-11.** "No two registered institutions carry the same name." The Registrar of PHEQA, same meeting.

**[paste them]:** the kinds of institution (university, university college, technical institute), owned by PHEQA; PHEQA's standards for new institutions, in versions, owned by the Registrar; the application fee, 1,200, owned by PHEQA's finance officer.

**[name the goal, for example 'apply for a licence']:** apply for a provisional licence.

## Example output

**Who.** The applicant, a person who intends to run a private institution. Also: PNIA (sign-in), the Payments block (fee).

**Starts and must be true.** The applicant decides to apply. The standards are in force; the fee is set.

**Guarantees.** Whatever happens: no application is recorded unless the applicant confirmed it and paid. On success: one application is recorded, with every particular and the version of the standards. If PHEQA rejects the application, the fee is refunded.

| Step | Who acts | What happens | Entries |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Applicant | Signs in to the self-service through PNIA | R-01 |
| 2 | Applicant | Asks to apply for a provisional licence | R-01 |
| 3 | System | Shows the standards in force, with their version | R-10 |
| 4 | Applicant | States the kind of institution, from the kinds of institution | R-10 |
| 5 | Applicant | Gives the proposed name and each particular, attaches the evidence of funding | R-10 |
| 6 | System | Checks every particular is given and no registered institution holds the name | R-10, R-11 |
| 7 | Applicant | Confirms and pays 1,200 through the Payments block | R-09 |
| 8 | System | Records the application as received that day and shows the date | R-01 |
| 9 | System | Sends the applicant an e-mail confirming receipt | ASSUMED |

**Assumptions to confirm:**

- Step 9: no entry asks for an e-mail. It would need an entry and a trusted address. Recommended to keep: applicants expect one.
- Evidence of funding as a document: assumed from R-10; needs the list of particulars in article 6.

## Reading the output

### 1. The steps trace to the entries

Steps 1 to 8 each name an entry, and every entry is served by a step. The fee is read from the setting, not invented, and the kind is taken from the agreed list. This is the part of the draft the analyst can keep almost as it stands.

### 2. The e-mail step is marked, then argued for

Step 9 is marked as assumed, which is what the prompt asks. But the draft then recommends keeping it because applicants expect one. That is the sentence the safeguard is written against: a step with no entry behind it is given a source or struck out, never kept because it sounds sensible. PHEQA holds no e-mail address it could trust, so the analyst strikes it and writes a question for the Registrar.

### 3. A refund promise nobody made

The success guarantees end with "if PHEQA rejects the application, the fee is refunded". No entry and no setting says so, and the draft did not mark it. A person ruling on each line catches it; read alone, it would become a promise PHEQA never made. Strike it, or send it to the finance officer as a question.
