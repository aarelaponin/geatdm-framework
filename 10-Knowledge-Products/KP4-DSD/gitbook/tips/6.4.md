## Example input

**[name of the ministry]:** MoEYS, the ministry of education, youth and skills.

**[paste the policy]:** an excerpt of MoEYS's policy on information and digital tools (invented for this example):

- §2. Every official document of the ministry names the officer responsible for it and the date on which it was approved.
- §3. Statistics the ministry publishes state their source.
- §4. A document not yet published is shared outside the ministry only with the approval of the Director concerned.
- §5. Personal data are processed only in systems the head of the ICT unit has listed as approved.
- §6. The head of the ICT unit keeps the list of approved digital tools.

**[paste the list]:** the register of what was asked; the records with their rules; the list of goals; the architecture; the shared groundwork; each goal in full; its screens; the interaction design; the application model.

## Example output

**MoEYS. Rules of use for an AI assistant in specification work (draft)**

| # | Rule | Rests on |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The assistant may draft every document on the list. | §6: "The head of the ICT unit keeps the list of approved digital tools." |
| 2 | A named officer of the Directorate of Higher Education accepts, amends or sets aside each draft. The officer's name and the date are written in the document. | §2: "Every official document of the ministry names the officer responsible for it and the date on which it was approved." |
| 3 | The assistant records each open question with the role that must answer it, and does not answer it. | Not covered by the policy |
| 4 | No figure enters a document without its source. | §3: "Statistics the ministry publishes state their source." |
| 5 | No personal data may be given to any digital tool outside the ministry. Unpublished documents go only to approved tools, with the Director's approval. | §5: "No personal data may be given to any digital tool outside the ministry"; §4 |

**Points the policy does not cover**

- How open questions are recorded and who answers them (rule 3).

## Reading the output

### 1. Rule 2 rests on the policy, and fits the method

§2 already says that a document names the officer responsible and the date. The rule of use only applies that to drafts, which is exactly the record the method asks for. Rule 3 is honestly marked "not covered", as the prompt asked.

### 2. The quotation under rule 5 is not in the policy

§5 says personal data are processed "only in systems the head of the ICT unit has listed as approved". The output quotes it as "no personal data may be given to any digital tool outside the ministry". That is a different, stricter rule, put in quotation marks. Reading each quoted paragraph in the policy itself, as the safeguard asks, catches it before adoption.

### 3. Rule 1 and rule 4 stretch their paragraphs

§6 is about who keeps the list of tools; it says nothing about which documents an assistant may draft. §3 is about published statistics, not about figures in a working document. Both rules may be sound, but the policy does not carry them. They belong on the list of points not covered, for the head of the unit to decide.
