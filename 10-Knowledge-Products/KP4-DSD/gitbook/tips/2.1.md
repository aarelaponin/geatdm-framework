## Reading the output

### 1. Six rows stand on the text

Six of the seven rows quote a sentence that is in the pasted excerpt, and each names the right body. The register column comes out full, as Progressa's catalogue shows: every service rests on the one register.

### 2. A row with an article that was never pasted

"Renew an institution's licence" quotes article 11(3). No article 11(3) was pasted, and the regulations, as Progressa has them, say nothing about renewal every five years. The assistant made up both the sentence and the service, so its count for the register and Payments columns is one too high. The safeguard catches it: for every row, open the article it quotes. Strike the row out, or put it to the Registrar of PHEQA as a question.

### 3. The credential is set aside correctly, and "today" is empty

The learner's credential is PDCA's duty, and PDCA's mandate was not pasted. The assistant was right to leave it out of the table. Paste PDCA's text and run again; do not add the row by hand. The "Today" column says "Not known" all through. Only the bodies can fill it: on paper, by letter, from a spreadsheet at the desk.
