## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa.

**[paste the story and its failures]:** the nine steps of "apply for a provisional licence" (sign in, ask to apply, read the standards, state the kind, give the particulars and the evidence of funding, checks, confirm and pay, record and show the date), with its failures: a document left out and a name already held (back to step 6), the fee not recorded (back to step 8, or failure).

**[paste them]:**

- **Records.** The register of institutions (name, kind, licence, standing); the applications PHEQA keeps.
- **Lists and settings.** The kinds of institution, owned by PHEQA; PHEQA's standards for new institutions, in versions, owned by the Registrar; the application fee, owned by the finance officer.
- **Other bodies.** PNIA's sign-in releases the person's name and an identifier for PHEQA's service. The Payments block returns a payment confirmation.

## Reading the output

### 1. The four sources are used as meant

The kind is chosen from the one list, the fee and the standards are read from the settings their owners keep, and the name is entered here with a reason. Step 7 is folded correctly: a system step gets no screen of its own. S3 names both failures it serves.

### 2. A phone number PNIA does not release

S3 claims the applicant's phone number is taken from PNIA's sign-in. The pasted list says the sign-in releases a name and an identifier, nothing else. This is the check the safeguard asks for: compare every source named with the lists you pasted. The value has no source; in Progressa it was struck out and a question went to the Registrar.

### 3. "NO SOURCE" is a question, not a gap

The number of students expected is marked correctly, with no source suggested. It may belong to a particular the regulations list, or it may have come from habit. It goes to the story's owner; nobody fills it in on the screen.
