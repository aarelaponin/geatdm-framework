## Example input

**[name of the application]:** MoEYS's application for higher education.

**[date]:** 15 March 2027.

**[paste the report]:**

- Summary: 4 entries of the register of what was asked; 2 traced to the end; 1 stops before generation; 1 stops before its acceptance check.
- R-03 The minister decides the licence → G-11, 3 screens, generated, check written. Traced to the end.
- R-07 A change of name needs the minister's approval → G-12, 2 screens, generated, check written. Traced to the end.
- R-08 The minister publishes the list of registered institutions → G-13, 2 screens, not yet generated, no check. Stops before generation.
- R-05 A person aggrieved may ask the minister to review a decision → G-14, 3 screens, generated, check not yet written. Stops before the acceptance check.

## Example output

**Status of the ministry's application, from the trace report of 15 March 2027**

Of the 4 things asked of the ministry's application (report of 15 March 2027), 2 are traced from end to end (15 March 2027): deciding a licence, and approving a change of an institution's name. Both are designed, generated and have an acceptance check.

Publishing the list of registered institutions in the Gazette is designed, with 2 screens (15 March 2027), but nothing is generated for it yet. At the current pace it should follow within two weeks.

Reviewing a decision of PHEQA at a person's request is generated, but its acceptance check is not yet written (15 March 2027). The check is for the supplier to write and the Director of Higher Education to accept.

Overall, the application is 50 per cent traced. Nothing in this status waits on a decision by the minister.

## Reading the output

### 1. Plain words, dated figures

The status names each of the four entries by what it does for the minister, not by its code, and puts the report's date beside the figures. Where each entry stops is read straight from the report. That is the status a minister can have checked by anyone.

### 2. "Within two weeks" is not in the report

The figure list says it comes from line R-08, but that line says only "not yet generated". The date was estimated from a "current pace" the report does not measure. Checked against the line it names, as the safeguard asks, it fails, and it comes out.

### 3. A figure that is derived, not stated

"50 per cent traced" is 2 out of 4, so it is arithmetic, not invention. But the report gives no percentage, and a percentage invites comparison with the supplier's own. Keep "2 of 4" and drop the percentage, or say that it is worked out from the report.
