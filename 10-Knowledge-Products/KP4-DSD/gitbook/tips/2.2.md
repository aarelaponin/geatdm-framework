## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa.

**[paste the text, keeping its article or paragraph numbers]:** four articles of the Higher Education Regulations of 2025:

- Art. 6(1): "An application for a licence shall be made to the Authority through its electronic self-service by the person who intends to operate the institution."
- Art. 9(2): "The Authority shall inspect the premises and facilities of the proposed institution before it makes a recommendation to the Minister."
- Art. 11(1): "The Minister shall decide whether to grant the licence, having regard to the recommendation of the Authority."
- Art. 22(1): "A person aggrieved by a decision of the Authority may request the Minister to review the decision."

## Reading the output

### 1. One thing per entry

The assistant split article 6(1) and article 9(2) into two entries each, and every split holds: who may apply is a separate constraint from where they apply. The tests in the fourth column are close to what Progressa's analyst wrote, though R-01's "PHEQA sees it" should say who sees it and what.

### 2. "Within 30 days" is not in the article

R-06 quotes article 22(1) with "within 30 days" added inside the quotation marks. The article gives the right and no period. The assistant filled the gap that Progressa's register records as an open question for the Director of Higher Education. The safeguard catches it: the analyst opens article 22(1), compares the words, and turns the period back into a question.

### 3. The owner column stays empty

The prompt left the owners blank, as it should. Your organisation names them: in Progressa, the Director of Higher Education for the review period and the Registrar of PHEQA for the questions about inspection.
