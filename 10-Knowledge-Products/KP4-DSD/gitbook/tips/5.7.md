## Example input

**[your administration]:** MoEYS, Progressa's ministry of education.

**[paste them]:** Two stories, as the officials agreed them:

- **G-12 Approve a change of an institution's name.** The minister opens the list of PHEQA's advices on a change of name that await a decision. She chooses one. She reads PHEQA's advice and the institution as PHEQA's register shows it. She approves or does not approve. The decision is recorded and sent to PHEQA.
- **G-13 Publish the list of registered institutions in the Gazette.** The minister opens the list for publication, read from PHEQA's register. She approves it for publication. The Gazette officer records the Gazette entry.

**[paste them]:** The minister: Licences; Names, the list and reviews; Suspension and cancellation; The Gazette; Requests for review. The Gazette officer: The Gazette.

1. Role: the minister.

4. Finished when: the approval is recorded and sent to PHEQA.
5. Run: not yet. Expected to pass, as the screens follow the story.

**Journey 2 — G-13 Publish the list in the Gazette**

1. Role: the minister, then the Gazette officer.
2. Start: The Gazette › List for publication.
3. Steps: (a) the minister opens the list, read from PHEQA's register. (b) She approves it for publication. (c) The Gazette officer signs in, downloads the approved list as a PDF and sends it to the Gazette. (d) She records the Gazette entry: number and date of issue.
4. Finished when: the Gazette entry is recorded against the approved list.
5. Run: not yet.

## Reading the output

### 1. Journey 1 follows the story

Every step of journey 1 is a step of the agreed story, with the start written as a menu category and an entry. It is the journey of this subtopic's worked example, and an officer could run it as written.

### 2. "Expected to pass" is not a run

Journey 1 adds a forecast to its run line. The safeguard says the line stays "Run: not yet" until the record of a real run replaces it, with the installation, the time and the result. Strike the forecast; a journey written and not run is reported as not run.

### 3. Step 2(c) is not in the story

The story says only that the Gazette officer records the entry. How the list reaches the Gazette, and in what form, was never agreed; the assistant filled it in. Mark step 2(c) "to settle", and the number and date of issue in 2(d) with it, and ask the Director of Higher Education.
