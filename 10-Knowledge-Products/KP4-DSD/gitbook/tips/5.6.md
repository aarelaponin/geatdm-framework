## Example input

**[your administration]:** PHEQA, Progressa's quality authority.

**[paste it]:** PHEQA's registration service. An applicant signs in and applies for a provisional licence through PHEQA's self-service. The application fee is 1,200. Until the Payments block is ready, the applicant pays into PHEQA's bank account and the finance officer records the evidence. MoEYS reads an institution's record from PHEQA's register when it opens a case.

**[paste it]:**

- Identity: PNIA's sign-in. Operator: PNIA.
- Registers: PHEQA's register of institutions. Operator: PHEQA.
- Payments: the government's Payments block, reached through its payer bank. Operator: the Payments block's operator.
- Data exchange: Linkup. Operator: PDGA.

## Example output

| # | What crosses | Direction | Body on the other side | Block | Who confirms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who the applicant is (PNIA's identifier for PHEQA) | In | PNIA | Identity | PNIA |
| 2 | Payment request: payer, payee, amount 1,200, currency, PHEQA's transaction number | Out | The applicant's bank | Payments | The central bank |
| 3 | Payment confirmation | In | The applicant's bank | Payments | The central bank |
| 4 | Evidence of a payment into PHEQA's bank account ⚠ | In | The applicant | None: goes round the Payments block | PHEQA's finance officer |
| 5 | An institution's record | Out | MoEYS | Data exchange (Linkup) | PDGA and MoEYS |

⚠ Row 4 goes round a block. It is the interim way to take the fee and should be planned to move to rows 2 and 3.

## Reading the output

### 1. The interim fee is named, not hidden

Row 4 is the honest one. The bank-account payment is how the fee is taken today, and the table marks it as going round the Payments block. That is what this subtopic asks for: the move to the block is now a row someone must plan and budget.

### 2. "The central bank" is not in the list

Rows 2 and 3 name the central bank as the body that confirms the fee. The pasted list names only "the Payments block's operator", and the prompt forbade inventing an operator. It may come from what the assistant knows of fast-payment systems. Confirming each row with the operator of its block is what will show who that is; until then, the cell reads "operator of the Payments block, to confirm".

### 3. Row 1 is half a crossing

The officers of PHEQA sign in too, through the same block, and the table has no row for them. Add one, or note that it is the same crossing for a different kind of person.

## What next

Take each row to the body named in its last column, then to the head of PHEQA's ICT unit for acceptance, and carry the table into the [software architecture](../toolkit/05_software-architecture.md). The crossings a person will use are then proved on the running service in [5.7](./5-7.md).
