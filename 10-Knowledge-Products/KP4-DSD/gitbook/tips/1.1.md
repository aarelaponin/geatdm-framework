## Example input

**[the ministry or agency]:** MoEYS, the ministry of education, youth and skills.

**[the service]:** the form on which MoEYS collects the particulars of private institutions for the list it publishes in the Gazette.

**[paste the note]:** the project's lessons-learned note, five points as written.

- "The form asked institutions for particulars that PHEQA already held in its register of institutions."
- "PHEQA told the project team in May that every institution has a register number. The form was designed in June with no field for it."
- "The national strategy says an institution gives a fact to the state once. No test of the form checked this."
- "For one institution the address on our form differed from PHEQA's letter, and we did not know which was right."
- "Go-live slipped by six weeks."

## Example output

| # | The problem, in the note's words | Place | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The form asked for particulars PHEQA already held | 1 | Nobody settled whether MoEYS may read PHEQA's register: "particulars that PHEQA already held in its register of institutions." |
| 2 | No field for the register number | 2 | A decision existed but never reached the design: "PHEQA told the project team in May … no field for it." |
| 3 | The once-only rule was not tested | 3 | The rule was written but nothing held anyone to it: "No test of the form checked this." |
| 4 | Two addresses for one institution | Cannot be placed from the note | The note says the addresses differed, not why: "we did not know which was right." |
| 5 | Go-live slipped by six weeks | 1 | The scope of the form was never settled, so rework delayed it: "Go-live slipped by six weeks." |

**Count:** place 1, two problems; place 2, one; place 3, one; cannot be placed, one.

## Reading the output

### 1. Rows 2 and 3 are earned

Each rests on a sentence that says the loss in so many words. Row 2 has the decision, its date and the design that missed it. Row 3 has the written rule and the missing test. A project team will recognise both at once.

### 2. Row 1 reads a cause into the note

The note says only that the form asked again. That nobody decided whether MoEYS may read the register is the likely cause, and it is the cause in this course's story, but the note does not say it. The quote supports the problem, not the place. Mark the row for the team to confirm.

### 3. Row 5 is forced, and the safeguard catches it

"The scope of the form was never settled" appears nowhere in the note. The assistant invented a reason so that a delay could sit at place 1. A delay is a result, not a place, and the note gives no cause for it. The safeguard says to keep it as the note states it: the row goes back to "cannot be placed from the note", and the count becomes one at place 1 and two not placed.

### 4. Row 4 was kept, rightly

The assistant did not guess why the addresses differed. That gap is the useful finding: the team must find out who was told of the move and who was not before anyone can say which place it belongs to.

## What next

Once the team has confirmed each row, take the problems at place 1 to [1.3](./1-3.md) and turn each into a question with a name on it. The problems at place 3 show where a check was missing; [1.4](./1-4.md) shows which document should have carried it.
