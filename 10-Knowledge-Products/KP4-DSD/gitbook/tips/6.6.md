## Example input

**[the new sector]:** health.

**[paste the examples]:** short excerpts of the education examples, one for each of the twelve documents. Among them:

- Register of what was asked: "R-03 The minister decides the licence (article 11 of the Higher Education Regulations)."
- Records: "The register of institutions, kept by PHEQA, one entry for each institution under an identifier like INS-00217."
- Shared groundwork: "Kinds of institution: university, university college, technical institute. Kinds of licence: provisional, full. States of a licence: granted, suspended, cancelled. Application fee: 1,200."
- Goal in full: "An applicant applies for a provisional licence through PHEQA's self-service."

**[paste the description]:** In Progressa, a private clinic must be licensed before it opens. The service is owed by the ministry of health, whose facilities unit receives applications and inspects. Applicants sign in through PNIA. (Invented for this example.)

## Reading the output

### 1. The form is kept and the obvious names are replaced

Every education name has gone: the facilities unit instead of PHEQA, a register of facilities instead of institutions, the education fee of 1,200 replaced by a marked place. The three places it lists are real questions for health officials.

### 2. Education's licence was carried across unmarked

"Provisional, full" and "granted, suspended, cancelled" are education's, set by the Higher Education Regulations. Nothing in the description says a clinic's licence has two kinds or three states, and row 7 builds on it. These should have been marked. The safeguard is what catches them: the sector's officials correct the drafts, and nothing here is health law until one of them confirms it.

### 3. The kinds of facility came from general knowledge

"Hospital, clinic, pharmacy" sounds right, and so it slips through. The description names clinics only, and a pharmacy may well be licensed by another body. The list belongs among the places to be read, with the question of who keeps it.
