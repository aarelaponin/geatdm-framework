## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa.

**[paste the list]:** G-01 Apply for a provisional licence (the applicant); G-02 Record the application fee (the finance officer of PHEQA); G-03 Record an inspection visit (the inspector); G-04 Recommend a decision to the minister (the registration officer); G-05 Appeal against a decision of PHEQA (the institution); G-11 Decide on a licence, G-12 Approve a change of an institution's name, G-13 Publish the list of registered institutions in the Gazette, G-14 Review a decision of PHEQA at a person's request (all the minister).

**[paste the register]:** R-01 An application is made through the self-service (art. 6(1)); R-02 PHEQA inspects before it recommends (art. 9(2)); R-03 The minister decides the licence (art. 11(1)); R-04 The register of institutions is open to the public (art. 14(3)); R-05 A person aggrieved may ask the minister to review a decision (art. 22(1)); R-06 An institution may appeal to PHEQA against its decision; R-07 A change of name needs the minister's approval (art. 26(1)); R-08 The minister publishes the list of registered institutions.

## Reading the output

### 1. The empty row is found

R-04 comes out exactly as in Progressa's check: nothing lets a member of the public look up an institution. It is written as a question for the Registrar of PHEQA, as the prompt asks.

### 2. A stretched mark hides the empty column

The mark for G-02 against R-01 rests on "usually": a habit of other services, not a sentence of article 6(1). Strike that mark and G-02 serves nothing. It should have been the question "was a fee asked for anywhere?". In Progressa the finance officer found the answer in a schedule under article 7 that nobody had given the analyst. Read the reason for every mark. A reason that cites no words of the entry is not a mark.

### 3. A goal the prompt was told not to propose

The output adds G-06 of its own accord. The safeguard applies: the result is a list of questions, not a change to either list. G-06 may well be right, and Progressa's Registrar did add it, but it is hers to add.
