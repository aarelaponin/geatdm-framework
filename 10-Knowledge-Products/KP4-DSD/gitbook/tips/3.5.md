## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa.

**[paste them]:** the nine steps of "apply for a provisional licence"; its failures (PNIA does not answer; a document left out; a name already held; the fee not recorded; the applicant leaves and returns two days later); and PHEQA's guarantee: no application is recorded unless the applicant confirmed it and the fee was paid.

**[paste the list]:** every page stamped "Screens of 'apply for a provisional licence', version 1.2, 14 November 2026".

- Page 1, S1 Ask to apply: the name from PNIA, earlier applications. Leads to page 2.
- Page 2, S2 Standards and kind. Leads to page 3, or to the failure ending.
- Page 3, S3 Particulars. Leads to page 4; side pages 3a (document left out) and 3b (name held).
- Page 4, S4 Confirm and pay. Leads to page 5; side page 4a (fee not recorded).
- Page 5, S5 Received. Leads to the success ending.

## Reading the output

### 1. The stamp is checked on every page

Each page opens with the version stamp, so a page produced from an older version of the screens is caught at once. The questions about the cost on page 2 and the ending of 3b are the kind that, in Progressa's session, changed the screens.

### 2. Page 5 answers its own question, wrongly

The prompt said not to answer, and page 5 does. The answer is also wrong: an institution receives its register number only when it is registered. Under the safeguard, a question the pages cannot answer becomes an open line with an owner. Strike the bracket before the session, so nobody reads it as the answer.

### 3. One question is for the builder

"Is the name check bound to the register's name field?" is written for the builder, not for the head of the registration desk. Rewrite it in her words: "Would you call this name taken?" Also, the failure where the applicant returns two days later has no page and no question; ask where it is.
