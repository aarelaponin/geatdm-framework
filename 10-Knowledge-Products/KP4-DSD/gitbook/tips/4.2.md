## Example input

**[your ministry]:** MoEYS, Progressa's ministry of education, youth and skills.

**[paste the list]:** the twenty matters PHEQA advises the minister on, one per line:

- a provisional licence for a university; for a university college; for a technical institute
- a full licence for a university; for a university college; for a technical institute
- extending a provisional licence; adding a programme to a licence
- suspending a provisional licence; suspending a full licence; ending a suspension
- cancelling a provisional licence; cancelling a full licence
- setting a cancellation aside; a cancellation the institution asks for
- a change of an institution's name; a change of premises; a change of governing board; a merger of two institutions; the closure of a campus

**[paste it]:** officers of MoEYS open the list of PHEQA's advice that waits for the minister's decision, and narrow it to one matter before they read the advice.

## Example output

**Licences (6)**
- provisional licence for a university, a university college, a technical institute
- full licence for a university, a university college, a technical institute

**Changes to a licence (4)**
- extending a provisional licence

- ending a suspension
- setting a cancellation aside

**Suspension and cancellation (4)**
- suspending a provisional licence
- suspending a full licence
- cancelling a licence
- a cancellation the institution asks for

**Changes to the institution (5)**
- a change of an institution's name
- a change of premises

**Hard to place**
- *ending a suspension:* Changes to a licence, or Suspension and cancellation
- *setting a cancellation aside:* the same two
- *the closure of a campus:* Changes to the institution, or Suspension and cancellation

## Reading the output

### 1. No step longer than nine

Four categories, none above six. Each name is plain, and the run marked the three matters it could have placed twice. That is the honest part of the answer: those three are where officers will disagree.

### 2. A value went missing

Count the matters: nineteen, not twenty. "Cancelling a provisional licence" and "cancelling a full licence" became one line, "cancelling a licence". The prompt forbade merging, and the list is changed only by its owner. Put both back before the proposal goes anywhere.

### 3. Let the officers decide where things go

The run split the suspension matters across two categories. Ask four or five officers where they would look for "ending a suspension" and "setting a cancellation aside". If they look under suspension and cancellation, move them there. In Progressa's example, the officers settled on three categories, of eight, seven and five.
