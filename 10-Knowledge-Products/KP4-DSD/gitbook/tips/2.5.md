## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa.

**[paste the text]:** the first draft of the goal *apply for a provisional licence*:

- Step 2. The applicant chooses the kind of institution: university or college.
- Step 4. The applicant uploads the business plan, a PDF of at most 10 MB.
- Step 6. The system shows the fee due, 1,000, and asks the applicant to pay it through the Payments block.
- Step 7. If payment is not received within 14 days, the application is closed.
- Step 8. The application is shown as "received", "under inspection" or "decided".

**[paste it, or write 'none yet']:** the kinds of institution (university, university college, technical institute), owner PHEQA; the states of a licence (granted, suspended, cancelled), owner the Registrar of PHEQA. No settings yet.

## Reading the output

### 1. Five items, and the code list caught

The assistant found every figure, period, list and set of states in the draft. Step 2's "university or college" is shown to disagree with PHEQA's list of three kinds. The draft would have left technical institutes with no choice to make.

### 2. The fee was given a source the draft never named

The draft names no source for 1,000. The assistant wrote "article 7" from its own guess about where fees are set, so the figure now looks traced. It is not: article 7 says PHEQA issues a schedule of fees, not what the fee is. In Progressa the 1,000 came from an old letter at the registration desk, and the fee in the schedule is 1,200. The safeguard applies: the row reads "no source: question", and the finance officer confirms the value.

### 3. A state set the shared groundwork lacks

Step 8 shows that an application has states of its own, which the shared groundwork does not hold. The question goes to the Registrar, not into the goal's text.

## What next

Write each confirmed value, with its owner and source, into the blank [shared groundwork](../toolkit/06_shared-groundwork.md), as in [PHEQA's](../examples/E2-5_what-every-goal-may-use.md). The goal then reads the setting when it is written out in full in [3.1](../module-3/3-1.md).
