## Example input

**[the service]:** the registration and licensing of private institutions.

**[the ministry or agency]:** PHEQA, the Progressa Higher Education Quality Authority.

**[paste each document, or its contents page and opening section]:** five project documents, each with its date and opening lines.

- **Terms of reference, 20 August 2026.** "Annex 1 reproduces the Higher Education Regulations of 2025 and the registration desk's written requests, as received."
- **Data dictionary, 14 September 2026.** "140 fields covering the application, the institution and the licence, with each field's type and length."
- **Solution design, 28 September 2026.** "PHEQA's application on its own installation. Sign-in through PNIA. MoEYS reads the register of institutions across Linkup. The fee is paid through the Payments block."
- **Business requirements document, 9 October 2026.** "BR-001 to BR-086. Each requirement below is traced from the approved solution design to the screen that meets it."
- **Test plan, 2 November 2026.** "Test scripts for each screen. Testing is complete when all scripts pass on the supplier's test server."

## Reading the output

### 1. Rows 1, 3 and 5 are earned

Each is matched on what the document holds, with a passage to show it. The data dictionary is rightly marked "partly": it lists fields but says nothing of whose each fact is, and no glossary or rules are quoted.

### 2. Row 2 is matched on words, not on what the document is

The business requirements document has numbered requirements, so it looks like a register. But its own opening says each requirement is "traced from the approved solution design". A list written from the design is not a register of what was asked; it is the list the design wrote. Row 2 should read "missing".

### 3. The safeguard changes the finding

This is the case the safeguard names: a document can contain the words of a register without being one, and only its owner knows how it was written. Ask the supplier's analyst who wrote it. If it came from the design, finding (c) is not a dating slip to fix by re-dating. It means the project has no register at all, and the design was never measured against anything PHEQA asked.

### 4. The test plan is not nothing

It matches none of the twelve, as the output says. It does show where checking ends today: on the supplier's server, not on a real task on PHEQA's installation, which is where row 12 is accepted.
