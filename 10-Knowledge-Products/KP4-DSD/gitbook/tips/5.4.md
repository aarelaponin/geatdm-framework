## Example input

**[your administration]:** MoEYS, Progressa's ministry of education.

**[paste it]:** The records of the supplier's first draft of MoEYS's design:

- **Officer:** given names, family name, national identity number, e-mail, role.
- **Institution:** register number, name, kind, licence state. Reason given: "faster screens".
- **Decision:** institution register number, institution name, the decision, date, signed by.
- **Request for review:** requester's given names, family name, national identity number, the decision of PHEQA concerned, grounds.

**[paste it]:**

- **PNIA:** who a person is. Reached through PNIA's sign-in, which gives each service its own identifier for the person.
- **PHEQA:** the register of institutions: register number, name, kind, licence, standing. Read through Linkup, operated by PDGA.

## Reading the output

### 1. The two copies the course warns about are found

The Officer row with the national number, and the Institution record with its "faster screens" reason, are the two copies this subtopic is about. The second is how MoEYS's copy keeps an old name after PHEQA writes the new one into the register.

### 2. A copy was ruled lawful

The prompt said not to decide whether a copy is lawful. The Decision row decides anyway: "acceptable and can stay". There may be a good case for recording the name as signed, but that is for the architect to settle with PHEQA and to record, not for the assistant. Keep it as a flag with an owner.

### 3. A national number was missed

The input puts a national identity number in Request for review as well. The run flagged only the Officer record, and its Request for review row lists the names but drops the number. Every national number should have been flagged; read the input again before trusting the "held in" line.

## What next

Take each flag to the body that keeps the fact, and correct the [entity model](../toolkit/03_entity-model.md) where the answer is to read rather than copy. The reading of PHEQA's register is then written down as a contract in [5.5](./5-5.md).
