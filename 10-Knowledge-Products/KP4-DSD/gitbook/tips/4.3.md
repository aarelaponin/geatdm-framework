## Example input

**[your ministry]:** PHEQA, the Progressa Higher Education Quality Authority (with MoEYS, whose minister decides).

**[paste the stories]:** the goals that touch a licence, one line each:

- PHEQA inspects the institution and recommends to the minister.
- The minister decides the licence; the registration officer records the decision.
- The minister suspends or cancels a licence; the registration officer records it.
- The minister ends a suspension, on PHEQA's advice.
- An institution appeals against a decision of PHEQA; PHEQA hears the appeal.
- A person aggrieved asks the minister to review a decision of PHEQA.

**[paste them]:** article 9 (PHEQA inspects before it recommends); article 11 (the minister decides a licence); article 22 (a person aggrieved by a decision of PHEQA may ask the minister to review it).

## Reading the output

### 1. Three states, written once

The states and their meanings match the stories, and the appeal is honestly marked NOT IN THE LAW: no pasted article gives PHEQA the power to hear one. That line goes to the legal officer as it stands.

### 2. Article 11 is stretched to cover suspension

Article 11 says the minister decides a licence. The run quotes it for suspending, cancelling and ending a suspension too. It may be what the regulations intend, but the pasted article does not say it. By the safeguard, each of those three rows should read NOT IN THE LAW until the legal officer names the article that does.

### 3. "The state it held before" became "granted"

A cancellation set aside returns the licence to the state it held before. A licence cancelled while suspended goes back to suspended, not granted. The run drew only one way back. Correct both rows before the screens are designed.
