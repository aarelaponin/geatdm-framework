## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa.

**[paste the supplier's list or data model]:**

- Institution: register number, name, kind, address. Has many Applications. Has 0..1 Licence per licence kind.
- Application: date received, licence kind, applicant name, applicant national number, standards version. Belongs to one Institution.
- Licence: kind, start date, state. Belongs to one Institution. Created by one Decision.
- Decision: date, ground, decided by. Settles one Application.

**[paste the list]:** PNIA (sign-in); MoEYS (reads the register across Linkup); PDGA (operates Linkup); the Payments block.

## Example output

**Sentences**

1. An institution is one private college or university that PHEQA knows of, under its register number.
2. An application is one request by one person, for one kind of licence, for one institution, on one day.
3. A licence is one permission for an institution to operate, of one kind, from one day.
4. A decision is one act that settles an application, with its date, its ground and who took it.
5. An institution may have many applications; each application is for one institution.
6. An institution may hold one licence of each kind, provisional and full.
7. Each licence is created by one decision.
8. Each decision settles one application.

**Facts and their keepers**

| Fact | Answer |
|---|---|
| Institution's address | Keeper not stated |
| Decision: decided by | Keeper not stated |
| Institution's name, kind | We keep it |
| Applicant's name | We keep it |
| Applicant's national number | We keep it |
| Licence state | We keep it |
| Standards version | We keep it |

## Reading the output

### 1. Plain sentences, ready to read aloud

There is no "entity" or "0..1" left. Sentences 2 and 4 are close to the one-line meanings in Progressa's records, and the two facts with no keeper are listed first, as asked.

### 2. The assistant passed on the national number

"Applicant's national number: we keep it" is a faithful reading of the supplier's list, and it is wrong. PNIA's sign-in gives PHEQA its own identifier for the person, and the national number must not be held. The applicant's name is not PHEQA's to keep either: it is taken from PNIA. The assistant read the list; it does not know the rules of Progressa's sign-in. The person who checks the read-back must.

### 3. The Registrar stops at sentence 6

Read aloud to the Registrar of PHEQA, sentence 6 does not survive. An institution whose provisional licence was cancelled may apply again and hold a second one. This is the safeguard at work: the sentence is a question for the records, not a wording to polish. The supplier changes the model: several licences of a kind over its life, one at a time.
