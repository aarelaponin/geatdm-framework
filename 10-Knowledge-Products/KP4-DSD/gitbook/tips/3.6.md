## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa.

**[paste the notes]:** review of the licence screens, version 1.2, Thursday 19 November 2026.

- Fee on S2 too, beside the standards. Analyst will change it by next Thursday.
- Name held by an institution whose licence was cancelled: still held? Registrar to decide, by 3 Dec.
- Governing board as one block of text: desk wants one member per line. Desk to confirm the list of roles first, then analyst.
- PNIA down: what may the screen say about trying again? Someone should ask PNIA for its service hours.
- Printed confirmation? Builder cannot tell from S5. Check the register?
- Application number is not the register number: head of the desk confirmed.

**[paste it]:** the supplier's analyst (owner of the screens); the supplier's builder; the head of the registration desk; the Registrar of PHEQA (convener).

## Reading the output

### 1. Half-decisions became lines

All five open points are lines, each in the notes' own words, and the one point that was settled is listed apart with the person who settled it. "By next Thursday" was turned correctly into 26 November, the Thursday after the meeting.

### 2. An owner the notes never named

Line 4 gives the question to the head of the ICT unit of PHEQA. The notes say only "someone should ask PNIA", and the head of the ICT unit was not even in the room. The safeguard catches this: an owner is named only if the notes name one. Line 4 goes back as "owner to be named at the review", and the Registrar names the owner.

### 3. Where the answers belong is half guessed

Line 5 follows the notes, and line 2 is rightly left "not stated". But "the architecture" for line 4 is nowhere in the notes; the prompt asked for this column only where the notes say. Treat it as a suggestion for the Registrar, not part of the record.

## What next

The Registrar names the missing owners and accepts the screens when every line is closed or allowed to stay open with its owner; compare the record with [E3-6](../examples/E3-6_the-review-three-people.md). Answers are written into the document that owns the fact, such as the [shared groundwork](../toolkit/06_shared-groundwork.md) or the [register of requirements](../toolkit/02_register-of-requirements.md), before the screens go to [4.1](../module-4/4-1.md).
