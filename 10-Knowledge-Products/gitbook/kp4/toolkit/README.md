# The blank instruments

A blank instrument is an empty form for one document of the method. Each has the same shape: what the document is for, who writes it and who accepts it, the lines to fill in, and the questions a manager asks before accepting it. The forms are plain: they hold the lines the method asks for and nothing more.

| Blank instrument | Document of the twelve | Who writes it | Taught in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [The catalogue of the sector's services](00_catalogue-of-services.md) | — | The team that commissions the services, for the whole sector | 2.1 |
| [The register of requirements](02_register-of-requirements.md) | 2 | An AI assistant extracts it; the analyst rules on each entry | 2.2 |
| [The entity model, with its glossary and its business rules](03_entity-model.md) | 3 | The analyst | 2.3 |
| [The use case model](04_use-case-model.md) | 4 | One named architect | 2.4 |
| [The software architecture](05_software-architecture.md) | 5 | The solution architect | 2.6, 5.4, 5.6 |
| [The shared groundwork: states, events, code lists and settings](06_shared-groundwork.md) | 6 | A person, with an assistant | 2.5, 4.3 |
| [One use case, written out in full](07_use-case-in-full.md) | 7 | An AI assistant writes it; a person rules on it | 3.1, 3.2 |
| [The screens of a use case](08_screens-of-a-use-case.md) | 8 | Whoever wrote the use case, beside it | 3.3, 3.4, 3.6 |
| [The interaction design](10_interaction-design.md) | 10 | One analyst writes it; the owner accepts it | 4.1, 4.2, 4.3 |
| [The review of the application model](11_application-model-review.md) | 11 | The person who reviews the model, from the list of assumptions and losses that the assistant reports | 4.4, 4.5, 4.6 |
| [The deliverables annex of the terms of reference for a supplier](A_deliverables-annex.md) | — | The ministry's procurement officer, with the team that commissions the service | 6.1 |

Three documents of the twelve have no blank instrument, because no person writes them as a blank. The customer's own documents are kept as received, the walk-through is produced from the screens, and the working application is generated from the application model.
