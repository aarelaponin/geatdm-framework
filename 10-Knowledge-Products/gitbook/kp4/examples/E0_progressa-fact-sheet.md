# E0 — Progressa's higher-education services: the fact sheet for this course

**Purpose:** The facts about Progressa that every worked example of this course relies on

**Public anchor:** None of its own; the bodies and blocks are those Education Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) Roadmap sets out for Progressa, and each example names its own public anchor

**Simulated case:** This is a simulated case for Progressa, a fictional country; every institution, name, date and figure in it is invented.

**What it shows.** The bodies, registers, services and building blocks that this course's worked examples use, with every name fixed once so that the thirty-seven examples tell one story.

**Form of this example.** A reference page. It names no real country, body or person. Where a fact comes from Education Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) Roadmap, it is given as Education Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) Roadmap gives it; where this course adds a fact, it is marked "new in this course".

## The story in one paragraph

Progressa wants a private college or university to deal with the state once, not twice. Today a private institution applies to the quality authority, PHEQA, for a licence, and later fills in the same particulars again at the ministry, MoEYS, when the ministry needs them. this course follows one project that fixes this. Two applications are built on a low-code platform: PHEQA's, which keeps the register of institutions, and MoEYS's, which reads that register across Linkup instead of keeping a copy. People sign in through PNIA. A fee is paid through the Payments block. this course uses this one project to show every document of the method, from the first request to the running service.

## The registers and the shared lists

| What | Kept by | What it holds | Who reads it |
|---|---|---|---|
| The register of institutions | PHEQA | One entry for each institution, under an identifier PHEQA gives it (in these examples written like INS-00217), with its name, its kind, its licence and its standing | PHEQA's own goals; MoEYS across Linkup; later PDCA |
| The kinds of institution | PHEQA | A shared code list of three: university, university college, technical institute | Every goal that asks for a kind |
| The kinds of licence | Progressa's Higher Education Regulations | Two: the provisional licence and the full licence | Every goal about a licence |
| The states of a licence | PHEQA's shared groundwork | Three: granted, suspended, cancelled | The goals that grant, suspend or cancel a licence |
| PHEQA's standards for new institutions | A setting owned by the Registrar of PHEQA | The criteria an application is judged against, in versions; an application keeps the version in force when it was made | The goal that applies for a licence, and the inspector |
| The application fee | A setting owned by PHEQA's finance officer | One amount, written once, read by every goal that needs it. In these examples it is 1,200 in Progressa's currency, an invented figure | The goal that takes the fee |

## The moves of a licence

A licence moves between its three states only on a decision someone is entitled to make, and the registration officer records every decision (subtopic 4.3; new in this course).

| Move | From and to | Who decides |
|---|---|---|
| Grant | none → granted | the minister decides a licence on PHEQA's recommendation |
| Suspend | granted → suspended | the minister decides |
| Cancel | granted or suspended → cancelled | the minister decides |
| End a suspension | suspended → granted | the minister decides, on PHEQA's advice |
| Set a cancellation aside | cancelled → the state it held before | a decision on appeal (PHEQA hears an institution's appeal) or on review (MoEYS reviews a decision of PHEQA) |

## The law

Progressa's Higher Education Regulations of 2025 (new in this course, invented) govern the registration and licensing of private institutions. this course's examples cite them by article: article 6 (an application is made through PHEQA's self-service, and carries the particulars the article lists), article 7 (PHEQA issues a schedule of fees), article 9 (PHEQA inspects before it recommends), article 11 (the minister decides a licence), article 14 (the register of institutions is public), article 22 (a person aggrieved by a decision of PHEQA may ask the minister to review it) and article 26(1) (a change of an institution's name needs the minister's approval). Notices are published in the Gazette, Progressa's official publication.

## The services

The seven higher-education services that PHEQA, MoEYS and PDCA owe, as Progressa's catalogue lists them (subtopic 2.1).

| Service | Owed by | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Register an institution | PHEQA | Enters a licensed institution in the register of institutions |
| License an institution | PHEQA, with the minister's decision | From the application for a provisional licence to the licence granted |
| Hear an institution's appeal | PHEQA | An institution appeals against a decision of PHEQA |
| Approve a change of an institution's name | MoEYS, with PHEQA writing the register | The institution asks; the minister approves; PHEQA records the new name |
| Publish the list of registered institutions | MoEYS | The minister publishes the list in the Gazette, read from PHEQA's register |
| Review a decision of PHEQA | MoEYS | A person aggrieved asks the minister to review a decision of PHEQA |
| Issue a learner's credential | PDCA | A graduate's credential, issued only for an institution whose licence is granted (the next service, subtopic 6.5) |
