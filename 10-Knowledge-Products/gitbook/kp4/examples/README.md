# The worked examples

These files show what each document of the specification-driven method looks like when it is filled in, using Progressa, the fictional country used across these knowledge products. They form one story: Progressa's quality authority for higher education, PHEQA, and its ministry of education, MoEYS, commission two applications so that a private institution gives its particulars to the state once. The first file fixes the facts every example uses. The others follow this course's subtopics in order: module 1 shows why services go wrong and the twelve documents that prevent it; module 2 shows the documents written before any design; module 3 shows one goal, *apply for a provisional licence*, designed in full and reviewed; module 4 shows the whole application settled once and described for the machine; module 5 shows the two applications as they will be generated, with the ministry reading the register across the data interface; module 6 shows a change of an institution's name, the trace report and the digital credential as the next service.

The examples are written for the manager who commissions a service, judges the supplier and convenes the review. Each one ends with what to look for before accepting the document it shows.

**How the files link.** Register entries carry identifiers `R-…` (E2.2, E2.4, E3.1), goals `G-…` (E2.4), screens `S…` (E3.3, E3.5, E3.6) and crossings `X-…` (E2.6). The goal *apply for a provisional licence* is G-01 in E2.4 and is written out in full in E3.1 to E3.6.

**Modules 4 to 6.** Their examples are carried by the scripts of their modules, and are taken from there as they stand, in the form of the examples of modules 1 to 3, by the program that renders the course guide. They are not edited in this folder: a correction is made in the module's script, and the file is written again.

**The figures.** Every figure, date and article number about Progressa is invented. Figures from public sources are quoted with their source.
