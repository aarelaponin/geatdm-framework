# Module 2 — Break the service down before you design it

**Written for:** the team that commissions the service and makes the case to the minister (Strategist register). **Videos:** 6.

**What it teaches.** The sector's catalogue and the blocks it shares; the register of what was asked; the records and their keepers; the goals; the shared registers; the architecture and its crossings.

**What you can do afterwards.** Commission the documents that are written before any design: the catalogue, the register, the records, the goals, the shared registers, the architecture.

**What it uses of the worked example.** Filled extracts of the documents written before design, one for each subtopic.

## Subtopics

| # | Subtopic | Class | Demonstration | Single message |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [2.1](2-1.md) | One catalogue of the sector's services, and the blocks they share | Core | — | List every service the sector's bodies owe once, with what each rests on, before you specify any one of them. |
| [2.2](2-2.md) | Write down what was asked before anyone designs | Core | — | One entry for each separate thing asked, in the customer's words, with where it was said. |
| [2.3](2-3.md) | The records the service keeps, and whose each one is | Core | — | For every fact: we keep it, we take it from another body, or we must not hold it. |
| [2.4](2-4.md) | Every goal named, and each tied to what was asked | Core | — | A service is broken into the goals people have in it; every goal serves an entry and every entry is served by a goal. |
| [2.5](2-5.md) | What every goal may use, and may not invent | Core | — | States, settings and code lists are written once and shared; a goal that needs something missing raises a question. |
| [2.6](2-6.md) | What the service is built on, and what crosses its boundary | Core | — | One document names the platform, the shared blocks it uses and every flow that crosses to another body. |

**Self-check.** [Four questions on what a manager decides](self-check.md), with the answer the module gives to each.
