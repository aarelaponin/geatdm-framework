# Module 6 — Run the method in your administration

**Written for:** the team that commissions the service and makes the case to the minister (Strategist register). **Videos:** 7.

**What it teaches.** What to require from a supplier; change; progress read from the work; the AI assistant and the person who rules; the next service; another sector; the method's own limits.

**What you can do afterwards.** Require the documents from a supplier, handle change, read progress from the work, use the AI assistant safely, and carry the method to the next service and another sector.

**What it uses of the worked example.** A change of an institution's name, the trace report, and the digital credential as the next service.
