## How the specifications are cited

References to the GovStack specifications are written as follows: REG §6.3.2.7 is the section of that number in the Registration specification; DRS-33 is the Digital Registries requirement of that number; ID 6.2-r2 is the second requirement under heading 6.2 of the Identity specification, which does not number its requirements. Every block is cited by its edition. The Digital Registries edition is an early release whose requirement numbers may change again, so a registry requirement is cited by its number and its short title together. A PAERA section is cited by its number, as in PAERA 2.3.
