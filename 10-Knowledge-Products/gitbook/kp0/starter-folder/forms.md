# The four forms

## Instruction — [the one task, as a question or a command]

*Save as `forms/instruction.md`.*

*Version 1 · [date] · kind of work: analysis / drafting / checking*

*Write it for a reader who knows nothing of your conversations. If a stranger could not carry it out from this page and the material, it is not finished.*

### 1. What it serves
Who will use the result, for what, and by when.

### 2. The material
Every document the work may use, by name and version. Nothing outside this list is evidence.

### 3. The task
What to do, step by step. Say what counts as each figure, for example what "caught" means.

### 4. How you will know it is done
The tests, numbered, each one a stranger could run. Write them before the work starts: a measure written afterwards is not a measure.
- T1 —
- T2 —

### 5. How the review will check it
The figure the review will work out again, and the different route it will take. Name it now, so that the report cannot borrow it.

### 6. How this work can fail while seeming to succeed
First, the faults of its kind, from the page in `kinds/`. Then the two or three ways that are particular to this task.

### 7. Out of scope
What not to touch, and which questions stay with you. *If something you need is not in the material, say so and name who could supply it, and do not supply it.*

### 8. Where the report goes
`2-reports/[the same name as this file]`, written from `forms/report.md`, with no verdict.

## Report — [the same title as the instruction]

*Save as `forms/report.md`.*

*Answers: [instruction file, version] · [date]*

### 1. The answer first
One paragraph: the figures or the finding, and what separates them. Say nothing wider than the tests below show.

### 2. The tests, one by one
For each test of the instruction: what was done, what came out, and where the evidence is.

| Test | Done | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | | | |

### 3. Found, not fixed
What came up that the instruction did not ask for. Report it, do not repair it.

### 4. What I could not establish
Each thing left open, and why.

### 5. Corrections to the instruction
Where the instruction was wrong or unclear, and what was done about it.

### 6. What was not finished

### 7. The outputs
Every file this work produced, by name.

*No verdict. A separate review decides.*

## Review — [the same title as the instruction]

*Save as `forms/review.md`.*

*Reviews: [report file] against [instruction file, version] · [date]*

**Verdict: accepted / not accepted.** One sentence saying why.

### 1. The tests, one by one
For each test of the instruction: met or not met, and the evidence you looked at yourself. A test that could not be run is not met.

### 2. One figure worked out again
The figure, the report's route, your route, and what you got. If the figures differ, name each difference.

### 3. The faults of the kind
What the page in `kinds/` asks a review of this kind to run, and what it showed.

### 4. The verdict, and what follows
- **Accepted:** what may now be built on it.
- **Not accepted:** the gaps, numbered, each one written so that it can be added to the instruction as version 2.

### 5. Found, not fixed
What you saw outside the tests. Fix nothing.
