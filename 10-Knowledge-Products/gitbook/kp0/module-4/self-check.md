# Self-check — Module 4

**Module 4 · Orchestrate: running it for days.** Answer each question before you read the answer beside it.

Four questions, drawn from the four pages. Questions 1 and 3 are multiple choice. Questions 2 and 4 ask for a short answer, which is right if it makes the point of the model answer. Three or four right means Ready; below three, read again the page named beside each question you missed.

| # | Question | Model answer | Drawn from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Your director asks on Friday how many of this week's pieces of work were accepted. What do you do? (a) Answer from memory: you ran them all. (b) Ask the last work chat. (c) Count the 'my ruling' rows in the log, and give the figure with the withdrawn ones named. (d) Ask each review chat again. | (c). Status is produced from the log, never remembered. A figure from memory is quoted afterwards as fact, and nobody can find where it came from. | [4.1](4-1.md) The log |
| 2 | A review of a drafting task finds that the draft supplied a figure the source does not carry. Your page of faults for drafting already says 'supply nothing the source does not carry'. What do you do with the finding, and what if the page did not say it? | The fault is already on the page, so it goes into version 2 of the instruction as a gap, and the page stays as it is. If the page did not carry it, add it to the page in the same sitting, so every later drafting instruction carries it. | [4.2](4-2.md) The faults you learn once |
| 3 | You hand your work to a colleague for two weeks. What goes into the handover note? (a) Every figure so far, so they need not look. (b) A full story of the work. (c) What you did that you should not have done, what is owed, findings in no report, and a few claims to check first. (d) Nothing: the log says it all. | (c). The note carries only what the files cannot. Figures belong in the files, where they can be worked out again; a story cannot be checked; and the log does not record what you regret or what is owed. | [4.3](4-3.md) Handing over |
| 4 | A colleague says: 'We ran it through the cycle, so the figure is true.' What does the pattern claim, and what does it not? | It does not make the output true, cheap or the same between runs. It makes each difference discussable and each failure countable: what was asked, what came back, how it was checked and who decided are all on file. | [4.4](4-4.md) Judgement |
