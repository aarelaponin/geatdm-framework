# Module 1 — Chat: get good answers in one chat

**Written for:** the public servant who commissions and accepts AI-assisted work and answers for it: a director, a head of unit, a technical secretary briefing the principal. No programming is assumed.

Module 1 teaches a public servant to get a good answer from an AI assistant in one chat, and to know what to do with it: why an assistant invents and why it agrees with you, the four-part prompt every AI usage tip in the series uses, how to read an output before using it, and what never goes into a chat and what stays yours. Each page carries the concept, what to do in the tool, a worked example on Progressa, an AI usage tip with a copy-paste prompt, and its sources.
