# E4 — Verification: the blank templates, and how Progressa's team used them

**Method step:** 4 of 9 — Verify the answers in facilitated sessions

**Public anchor:** PAERA version 1.0 (GovStack, 2024), section 5.4, whose eight-step procedure (criteria, tools, conduct, analysis, recommendations, action plan, monitoring, continuous improvement) is the nearest public procedure

**Simulated case:** This is a simulated case for Progressa, a fictional country; every institution, name and figure in it is invented.

**Form of this example.** The templates are the team's own and are shown blank. A short section at the end shows three rows Progressa's team filled in, so that the reader can follow one desk result through to a finding.

## What this step does

A questionnaire answer is what a body says about itself. A desk result is what the public record suggests. Neither is yet a finding. In this step the facilitator checks both against documents and against working systems, and settles each point where they disagree. Only a verified position goes forward to scoring in step 5.

## Roles

| Role | Who holds it | What the role does |
|---|---|---|
| Facilitator | A member of the assessment team | Plans and runs the sessions, keeps the evidence, writes the verified positions |
| Respondent team lead | The head of the unit that answered the questionnaire | Brings the right people and documents; signs off the verified positions for the unit |
| Section leads | Staff of the responding unit, one per questionnaire section | Answer the clarification questions for their section |
| Focal points | One person in each other body named on the frame page | Confirm or correct statements about their own body's systems |
| Validation chair | A senior official named by the sponsor | Chairs the final validation workshop and closes disputed points |

## The verification sequence

| Activity | Purpose | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Initial questionnaire | Receive the signed answers of step 3 | Answers with their documents |
| 2. Document review | Read every document the answers name; compare with the desk results of step 2 | A list of points that agree, disagree or lack evidence |
| 3. Clarification interviews | Put each disputed point to the section lead who owns it | Interview notes, one per point |
| 4. Technical workshops | See the systems working: a screen, a report, a log | Observed evidence |
| 5. Synthesis | Write the verified position for every point, with its evidence | The reconciled-response table |
| 6. Final validation | Present the verified positions to all bodies; close what is still disputed | Validated positions, ready for scoring |

## The evidence ladder

When sources disagree, the higher level wins. A verified position needs either one source at level 1, or two independent sources at levels 2 or 3.

| Level | Kind of evidence | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A system seen working | A demonstration, an export, a log |
| 2 | An official document in force | A law, a signed regulation, an approved budget |
| 3 | Official statistics | A published yearbook |
| 4 | An internal report | A unit's annual report, a project report |
| 5 | A statement in an interview | What an official says in a session |

Where two sources at the same level still disagree, the facilitator records both, marks the point disputed and brings it to the final validation workshop.

## How Progressa's team used the templates (illustrative)

Four sessions were held in week 8 of the assessment: V-01 with the MoEYS statistics unit, V-02 with PDGA, V-03 with PNIA and V-04 with the MoEYS ICT unit.

| Point | Desk result (step 2) | Questionnaire answer (step 3) | Verified position | Evidence and its level | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AF-DAT-02 | PEMIS appears to hold learner-level data for secondary schools | PEMIS holds district totals only (answer 2.2) | PEMIS holds totals by district, grade and sex. The learner lists some secondary schools keep are spreadsheets outside PEMIS | PEMIS shown in session V-01, data tables listed (level 1) | corrected |
| AF-INT-01 | MoEYS does not appear among Linkup's members | Not asked in the Digital Data questionnaire; the Interoperability questionnaire confirms it | MoEYS is not a member of Linkup and runs no service on it | Member list exported by PDGA in session V-02 (level 1) | verified |
| AF-IDN-02 | No education service appears to check people through PNIA's sign-in | MoEYS confirms it does not | Only PNEA reads persons from PNIA, through the read by national number on Linkup; no education service is a client of PNIA's sign-in | PNIA's service log for 2025 and its list of sign-in clients, shown in session V-03 (level 1) | verified |

These verified positions are the evidence behind findings F-DAT-2, F-INT-1 and F-IDN-2 in E5.
