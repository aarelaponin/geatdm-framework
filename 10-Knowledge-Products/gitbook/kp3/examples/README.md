# The worked examples

These nine files show what each step of the method for assessing a country's digital public infrastructure and writing its roadmap produces, using Progressa, the fictional country used across these knowledge products. They form one story: Progressa's desk assessment and questionnaires lead to one maturity table, its gaps are registered and ranked, the roadmap addresses those gaps, the investment case costs the roadmap, and the validation round answers comments on both.

| File | Step | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| [E1_five-domain-frame.md](E1_five-domain-frame.md) | 1. Frame the assessment | The five domains read from PAERA, and Progressa's one-page scope |
| [E2_automatic-desk-assessment.md](E2_automatic-desk-assessment.md) | 2. Run the automatic desk assessment | The question bank and workflow, and a desk run on Progressa's public record |
| [E3_domain-questionnaire.md](E3_domain-questionnaire.md) | 3. Put one questionnaire to each domain | The blank Digital Data questionnaire with its guides, and a completed extract |
| [E4_verification-templates.md](E4_verification-templates.md) | 4. Verify the answers | Blank verification templates, and three points Progressa's team verified |
| [E5_domain-report-and-maturity-table.md](E5_domain-report-and-maturity-table.md) | 5. Score and report | The scoring rule, the Digital Data domain report, and the maturity table and findings for all five domains |
| [E6_gap-register.md](E6_gap-register.md) | 6. Consolidate and rank the gaps | The gap report outline, the priority rule, and Progressa's gap register |
| [E7_roadmap.md](E7_roadmap.md) | 7. Write the roadmap | The roadmap's structure, with the first horizon in full and the component register |
| [E8_investment-case.md](E8_investment-case.md) | 8. Lay out the investment case | The four sheets: cost by component, cost by domain, funding sources, returns |
| [E9_validation-response-matrix.md](E9_validation-response-matrix.md) | 9. Validate and revise | The four-phase revision with its AI prompts, and Progressa's response matrix |

**How the files link.** Findings carry identifiers `F-…` (E5), gaps `G-…` (E6), roadmap components `C-…` (E7), investment lines `INV-…` (E8) and comments `CM-…` (E9). Each gap names its findings, each component names its gaps, each investment line names its component, and each comment names what it refers to, so a reader can follow any budget line back to the evidence behind it.

**The scale.** Maturity is scored on the five stages of the UNDP Digital Development Compass — Basic, Opportunistic, Systematic, Differentiating, Transformational — on its range of 0 to 5. The five domains are PAERA's. How the scale is applied to the domains is the team's own rule, stated in E5.

**The figures.** Every figure about Progressa is invented and every table of figures is illustrative. Figures from public sources are quoted with their source and year.
