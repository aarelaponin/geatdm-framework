# Frameworks and standards

Every framework and standard this guide cites is public, and each is given here with the address where it can be read. They are arranged in the five kinds that section 4.2 of the terms of reference names. The short name in bold is the one the subtopic pages use.

## Frameworks for digital public infrastructure

What DPI is, how mature a country's DPI is, how to approach it and how to keep it safe and inclusive.

- **UNDP Compendium** — UNDP, *Accelerating the SDGs through Digital Public Infrastructure: A Compendium of the Potential of Digital Public Infrastructure*, 21 August 2023 — https://www.undp.org/publications/accelerating-sdgs-through-digital-public-infrastructure-compendium-potential-digital-public-infrastructure
- **UNDP Compass** — UNDP, *Digital Development Compass — Methodology*, web page — https://digitaldevelopmentcompass.undp.org/methodology
- **UNDP Playbook** — UNDP, *The DPI Approach: A Playbook*, 21 August 2023 — https://www.undp.org/publications/dpi-approach-playbook
- **Safeguards Framework** — United Nations, *Universal DPI Safeguards Framework: A Guide to Building Safe and Inclusive DPI for Societies*, September 2024, developed under the stewardship of the Office of the Secretary-General's Envoy on Technology and UNDP. ITU is a member of its consultative group of international organisations, not its author. — https://www.dpi-safeguards.org/framework

## Enterprise architecture frameworks

Section 2.3 of PAERA, on the role of enterprise architecture. The frameworks themselves are taught in the enterprise architecture course; this course adds none of its own, because a roadmap puts in order the architecture that the enterprise architecture method designs.

- **PAERA** — GovStack, *Public Administration Ecosystem Reference Architecture (PAERA)*, version 1.0, dated 25 May 2024. Cited by section. — https://paera.govstack.global/

The enterprise architecture course in this knowledge base is [Developing a Gov Enterprise Architecture (GEA)](../kp1/README.md).

## Interoperability standards

How public bodies exchange data: the data exchange layer and its rules.

- **X-Road documents** — NIIS, X-Road release 7.7.0: Architecture (ARC-G), Security Server User Guide (UG-SS), Message Protocol for REST (PR-REST), Operational Monitoring Protocol (PR-OPMON) — https://github.com/nordic-institute/X-Road/tree/7.7.0/doc
- **IM** — GovStack, Information Mediator Building Block specification, version 1.1.1 — https://specs.govstack.global/information-mediator
- **EIF** — European Commission, European Interoperability Framework, whose four layers the interoperability course teaches — https://interoperable-europe.ec.europa.eu/collection/iopeu-monitoring/european-interoperability-framework-detail

The interoperability course in this knowledge base is [Building a Government Interoperability Framework (GIF)](../kp2/README.md).

## Reference architectures for digital government

- **PAERA** — GovStack, *Public Administration Ecosystem Reference Architecture (PAERA)*, version 1.0, dated 25 May 2024. Cited by section. — https://paera.govstack.global/
- **REG** — GovStack, Registration Building Block specification, the site's default edition (site label "23Q4", version history to 1.0) — https://specs.govstack.global/registration

- **ID** — GovStack, Identity Building Block specification, "Version 2.0; December 2025" — https://specs.govstack.global/identity
- **PAY** — GovStack, Payments Building Block specification, "Version 3.0; December 2025" — https://specs.govstack.global/payments
- **IM** — GovStack, Information Mediator Building Block specification, version 1.1.1 — https://specs.govstack.global/information-mediator
- **CON** — GovStack, Consent Building Block specification, version 1.3.0 — https://consent.govstack.global/
- **MSG** — GovStack, Messaging Building Block specification (site label "messaging-23Q4.1", version history to 1.0) — https://specs.govstack.global/messaging
- **GovStack Architecture** — GovStack, Architecture specification, edition 2.1.0 — https://specs.govstack.global/architecture/

## How the specifications are cited

References to the GovStack specifications are written as follows: REG §6.3.2.7 is the section of that number in the Registration specification; DRS-17 is the Digital Registries requirement of that number; ID 6.2-r2 is the second requirement under heading 6.2 of the Identity specification, which does not number its requirements; PAY 6.4-r3 is formed the same way for Payments. Every block is cited by its edition.
