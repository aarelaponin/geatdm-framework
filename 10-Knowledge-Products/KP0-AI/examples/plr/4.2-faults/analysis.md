# The discipline for analysis — the page of the kind (PLR)

*One of the PLR project's four pages, read into every instruction of the kind and every review of it
by reference and never retyped (section 11 of the procedure, "The kinds of work of a project"). An
instruction of this kind names it in its read-first list and in its section 7, adding only the two or
three failures particular to the task; the review instruction names it too.*

**The kind.** Analysis is the work that answers an open question from documents, and stops at the
answer: what identifies a learner (PLR-003), the requirements set against the goals. It writes a
paper with a recommendation, never a decision; the decision is the person's (section 5 of the
procedure).

**Where its faults come from.** `DISCIPLINE-application-design.md`, items 2, 3, 4 and 9, and
`DISCIPLINE-the-methods-own-work.md`, items 2 and 5, as the PLR project's analysis meets them.

**How this page grows.** A fault a review finds that this page does not carry is added here by the
orchestrator in the same act that appends the review's gaps to the instruction as its next version
(section 6.4 of the procedure).

---

## 1. What a session of this kind refuses

1. **To call a proposal a decision, or to restate a governing document instead of quoting it at its
   line.** The Education Sector Plan, the Data Protection Act and the brief are quoted where the
   paper rests on them, with the path and the line.
2. **To put the case for one position without the passages that make the other strongest.** Where a
   question has positions (A and B of PLR-003), each is stated at its strongest, from the documents,
   before either is judged; and the judgements of the positions are kept in one table, position by
   test, so that a fact counted against one and not the other can be seen.
3. **To report a count or a check over a set its selection does not reach.** The set a test of
   success names — every requirement, every goal — is listed first from its own source, and the
   round's selection is set against it as a set before a zero or a total is reported.
4. **To cite a source at a line where it does not hold**, or to write a figure into the paper from
   anything but the line that holds it, read at the moment the paper is written.
5. **To write a paper that contradicts itself or the brief it answers**: a figure given twice is the
   same figure both times, and a departure from the brief is said, with its reason.

## 2. What a review of this kind runs before it judges

- Every citation read at its path and line, by the reviewer, against the sentence that cites it.
- The set each test of success names, listed again from its own source and compared as a set with
  what the paper covers.
- The table of positions read test by test: every fact a verdict rests on looked up in the other
  position's cells.
- One figure of the paper worked out again by a route the paper did not use.

## 3. What the orchestrator verifies before it issues an instruction of this kind

By reading, which is in its role:

- that the question is one the person has not already decided, and that the instruction asks for a
  recommendation, not a decision;
- that every document the analysis rests on is named among the inputs, by path, so that the ledger
  pins it;
- that each set a test of success counts is named with the source it is listed from.
- that every program the instruction has the round run under `round_record.py run` is named with
  an interpreter that can trace it and holds the libraries it needs — on this computer
  `~/.pyenv/versions/3.11.14/bin/python3`, never the bare `python3` (3.7.9), which traces nothing
  (PLR-020: a cause the orchestrator found behind the gaps of the kind item of PLR-001 to PLR-003);
- that every file the programs of a skill named in the instruction read lies under a root, or a
  `--read` or `--read-from`, of the `round_record.py start` the instruction writes out, so that the
  criterion on the record can be met (PLR-020: a cause the orchestrator found behind the gaps of the kind item of PLR-001 to PLR-003).
