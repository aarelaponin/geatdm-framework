# Drafting — the faults it refuses

**The kind.** Drafting writes a document that others will build on or receive. Examples are terms of reference, a decree, a business case or a note to a senior reader. Its content is settled by work already accepted. What is open is only whether it is written right.

*How this page grows: when a review finds a fault that is not on this page, add it here. Every later instruction of this kind then carries it.*

## 1. What the work chat refuses
1. **To supply a fact, a rule or a requirement the source does not carry.** It is a gap. It is raised against the source document, never filled in here.
2. **To close a review's gap only for the examples it names.** A gap that names three members of a set states a rule. The next version applies it to every member, and says how many still fail.
3. **To write for a senior or outside reader in working language.** The document uses the reader's words: no internal codes, no file names, no jargon of the project.
4. **To leave out a limit the record names, or to say more than the record carries, without saying so.** Every limit that an accepted document names is either in the draft or in the report's list of what was left out, with the reason. A limit of one step is stated as a limit of that step, not of the whole: a search that misses a child does not mean the system cannot find the child, if another step may find it. The draft's claims are listed, each with its source, before the draft is written.

## 2. What the review chat runs
- Every claim of the draft looked up at its source.
- Where a test asks for every member of a set, such as every limit the record names, the review lists the set itself from the same sources. It compares the two lists row by row and counts what is missing.
- For a document for an outside reader, a read for internal words and codes.

## 3. What you check before you issue the instruction
- The documents the draft rests on are accepted, and at the version you name.
- Where a test asks for every member of a set, the instruction names where the set is drawn from, and asks for it to be listed and counted before the draft.
