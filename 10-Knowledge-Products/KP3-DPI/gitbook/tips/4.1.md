## Example input

**[the ministry]:** the Ministry of Education, Youth and Skills (MoEYS). **[country X]:** Progressa. **[the people the sector serves]:** every learner from primary school to university.

**[the proposal]:** MoEYS would issue a Progressa Learner Card to every learner. District staff would enrol learners at school with a photo and a fingerprint, using kits sent to each school. A new identity unit inside MoEYS would run a central learner identity database, with its own back-up site, print the cards, open enrolment desks at district offices and run a campaign for parents and head teachers.

**[the authority]:** the Progressa National Identity Authority (PNIA).

**[its services]:** the National ID since 2018, 78% adult coverage, with enrolment offices, kits and staff across the provinces; an e-KYC sign-in since 2024, to which a service connects as a registered client; a read of a person by national number on Linkup, open to the examinations authority only. PNIA issues identity numbers only at 16.

**For the finance ministry**

- Four of the six categories would be paid a second time, and two in part. Using PNIA, MoEYS carries one cost: connecting its services and testing them.
- The real gap is children below 16. It is closed by a learner number in the learner register, joined to PNIA's identifier at 16, not by a second identity system.
- No amount is given here. A costing of both options should come from the budget records before any decision.

## Reading the output

### 1. The duplication is earned

Every "yes" rests on something the input says PNIA already runs: staff, offices, kits, a central system and a sign-in services can join. The table follows the study's six categories in its own order and gives no figure, as the prompt asks.

### 2. The second bullet goes beyond the table

The proposal to give children below 16 a learner number joined later to PNIA's identifier is the roadmap's answer (C-07), not something the prompt asked for. It is right for Progressa, but the model offered a design in a cost table. Keep the bullet only if the Architect can point to the component it comes from.

### 3. Check the two "partly" rows

The credential and the school kits are marked "partly" because PNIA does not reach children or schools today. Ask PNIA whether its enrolment kits could serve schools, and how birth registration (71%) would anchor a learner below 16. Those answers decide whether "partly" becomes "no".
