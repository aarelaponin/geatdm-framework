## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa.

**[the programme]:** the Progressa scholarship test programme, the pilot behind the roadmap's scholarship beacon. The paying body is MoEYS. Its account is a test account at the payer bank, which the build will name; the currency is Progressa's own, replaced in tests by the ISO test code. Learners verified in the learner register (PLR) are paid once a term into a bank account. The service that sends the payments is the learner registration service on the Registration block, run by MoEYS.

## Reading the output

### 1. The values are test values and agree with the specimen

Every identifier, account, amount and address is marked [test], and they are the same as in the build plan's payment specimen. The beneficiary is keyed on a learner number, so no national number and no real account enters the draft.

### 2. The policy was left open, rightly

The model did not invent a policy identifier and marked it [confirm]. The specification names the field, but its value comes from the programme's rules, which nobody has written yet. That is a gap in the programme, not in the draft.

### 3. Check which service sends the payments

The draft follows the specimen and names the learner registration service as the source. A scholarship service of its own would be a different source, with its own identifier. Decide this before the beneficiary is onboarded, because the account mapper refuses the same identifier twice from the same source.
