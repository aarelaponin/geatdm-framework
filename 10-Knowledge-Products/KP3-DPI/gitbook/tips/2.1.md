## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa.

**[the registering body]:** the primary school, under the Ministry of Education, Youth and Skills (MoEYS).

**[the procedure]:** A parent collects a paper enrolment form at the school office in the term before the school year starts. The parent fills it in and brings it back with the child's birth certificate; a learner who transfers also brings a letter from the previous school. The school clerk checks that every box is filled and returns incomplete forms to the parent. The head teacher reads each form, admits the child or refuses (no place, or the child is under three) and signs the form. The parent receives a stamped admission slip. Each term the head teacher keys the admitted learners into the district EMIS, with its own learner number. The form itself is a template the district education office printed in 2019.

**Steps I could not place:**

- Keying admitted learners into district EMIS each term: it is the write to a register, but the register is a district list, not an authoritative one.
- The birth certificate shown at the counter: checked by eye, with nothing recorded; no capability holds a check that leaves no trace.

## Reading the output

### 1. The decisions are found where they hide

The clerk's return of an incomplete form is a decision, send back for correction, and the table names it. That is supported by the input, and it matters: in the block, completeness on sending removes most of that work before anyone sees the file.

### 2. "Office that takes it today" is not "office that should take it"

The table rightly records the head teacher as deciding today. Read too fast, it suggests the head teacher is the registrar of the new service. Progressa has no legal basis yet for a learner register (G-01), so no text names the registrar; until it does, the deciding office is an open question, not a row to copy into the configuration.

### 3. The two unplaced steps are the design questions

The EMIS keying is where the record is written after approval, and the birth certificate is where identity is checked. Both are answered later in this module: the write to the learner register, and the comparison with PNIA's record. Check the termly census return too; the description did not mention it, and it may be a second register.

## What next

The sorted steps become the registration brief in the specification's terms, from which [2.3](./2-3.md) drafts the service description. The open question of who decides goes to the legal basis, gap G-01 of the [gap register](../examples/E6_gap-register.md).
