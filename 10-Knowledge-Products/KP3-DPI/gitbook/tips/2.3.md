## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa.

**[the brief]:** Service: Progressa learner registration. One registration, learner registration; entity in charge: PLR, the Progressa Learner Registry. Subjects: mandatory for a learner aged three or older entering a Progressa school. Determinant: the learner transfers from another school, which requires a transfer letter from the previous school. Data: given names, family name, date of birth, school (from the list of Progressa schools), grade, transfers from another school (yes or no), and the identifier PNIA gives this service at sign-in. No fee. Result: a registration confirmation carrying the learner's register number. Screens in order: guide, applicant form (with the button "Sign in with PNIA"), documents, payment (switched off, no fee), send.

**[product name]:** not yet chosen.

**[format]:** none chosen; write it in the specification's own terms, as YAML.

## Reading the output

### 1. The brief is carried across whole

The registration, its entity in charge, the determinant and its document, the result and the five screens in order with payment switched off all come from the brief. The head names the format and the edition, as the prompt asks. That agrees with Progressa's specimen description.

### 2. The processing part is invented

The brief said nothing on processing, so the run added a single registrar. The specimen has three roles: an automated check of the file as sent, the registrar, and an automated write to the register. A draft with one role would import and look complete, and the write would be missing. The ASSUMPTION mark is the only thing that catches it.

### 3. Nothing here has been imported

With no product chosen, this is a description in the specification's terms, not a file any product reads. It must be rewritten in the product's format, imported into a test installation and checked: the service listed, its screens in order, a transferring and a non-transferring learner asked for different documents.
