## Reading the output

### 1. The sessions follow the owners

Each session meets the body that answers for its domain, and asks for evidence a facilitator can see working: a screen, a member list, a log. That is the kind of evidence that settles points later.

### 2. Influence and timing are guesses

"High" or "medium" came from one line per institution. Three weeks for a questionnaire assumes each unit starts at once; the respondent's process alone takes about 13 working days.

### 3. Governance has no session of its own

GOV is folded into V-02 with PDGA, and the legal unit and the budget department meet no one in week 8. Ask the coordinating body whether the education act and the budget need their own session.
