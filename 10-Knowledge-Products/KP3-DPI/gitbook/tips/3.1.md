## Example input

**[1] Articles of the education act (the education act of 2011, invented for Progressa).** Art. 14: every head teacher keeps an admission register of the learners of the school. Art. 15: the head teacher sends an annual return of enrolment to the district education office. Art. 31: school records are kept for as long as the minister directs. The act has no clause on a learner register or on digital records.

**[2] The ministry's brief (MoEYS, invented).** B1: PLR becomes the single record of every learner, from primary school to university. B2: PLR keys each learner on a learner number of its own and keeps the identifier PNIA gives it, never the national number. B3: records reach PLR only after a PLR officer approves them. B4: a data steward is named at MoEYS. B5: until the law is amended, PLR runs in one pilot province, with parents' consent.

**[3] Services and their needs.** Learner registration: creates and updates records. PEMIS: learner counts by school and grade. PNEA: candidates for examination. Scholarship pilot: an eligible learner, verified. A parent: their own child's record.

**Points the owner must confirm**

- The legal basis: nothing in the act names PLR; the brief's pilot rests on parents' consent alone.
- Retention, and the treatment of a learner who leaves, transfers or completes.
- The fields each reader may see.
- Whether a child below the age of identity issue can have a record without PNIA's identifier.

## Reading the output

### 1. The gaps are named, not filled

The run did not invent a retention period. It turned Article 31 into a question and flagged that no article names PLR, which agrees with the missing legal basis found in the assessment (G-01). That is the charter doing its job.

### 2. Article 14 is stretched

The run cites Article 14 for the register's content. That article is about a school's own admission register, not a national one. The citation looks like support but is not; it should read "no article — brief B2" and go to the legal unit.

### 3. The status field and the readers' fields are unchecked

"Status" appears in the content but nothing in the input asks for it. The fields each reader needs were taken from one line per service; each service owner should confirm them before the access rules are drafted.
