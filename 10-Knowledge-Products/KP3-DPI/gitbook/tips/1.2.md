## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa.

**[paste the list]:**

- Education act of 2011: names PEMIS as the source of education statistics.
- Data protection act of 2019: applies to all public bodies.
- Digital transformation roadmap 2024–2030: draft, awaiting cabinet.
- PDGA: owns Linkup and sets the national exchange rules.
- MoEYS planning directorate: education strategy and budget.
- PEMIS: totals by district, grade and sex.
- PLR: enrolment records for some learners, served on Linkup to PNEA.
- PNEA: examination candidate records.
- Linkup: X-Road pilot since 2025; members PNEA, PLR, PNIA.
- PayPro: fast-payment system.
- PNIA: national identity since 2018, 78 per cent of adults; sign-in since 2024.
- District education offices: help parents in person at school offices.
- School connectivity records: some provinces only.

## Reading the output

### 1. The map follows the frame

Every item lands where the frame page of E1 puts it, with PayPro under INT as the payment rail and PNEA's records under DAT. Leaving PLR in two domains is right: E1 assigns DAT to the statistics unit with PNEA, and INT to PDGA, so both must be asked about it.

### 2. "Not found" means "not on the list"

The third column reads like findings, but it only says what a 13-line list did not mention. Some entries are true for Progressa; the assistant could not know that from the list. Treat the column as questions for the desk assessment, not as gaps.

### 3. Settle the double placements with their owners

Put PLR to the MoEYS statistics unit and to PDGA together: what does it hold, under what law, and who calls it? The answer decides which questionnaire asks about it first.
