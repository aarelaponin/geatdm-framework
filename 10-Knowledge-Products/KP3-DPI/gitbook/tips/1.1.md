## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa.

**[paste the list]:**

- **National identity and sign-in (PNIA).** Identity numbers issued since 2018, 78 per cent of adults covered, issued from age 16. A sign-in for people since 2024. One service on Linkup, a read of a person by national number, called by PNEA alone. API documented. Anchored in the Civil Registration Act. Funding not stated.
- **PayPro.** Fast-payment system run by the central bank, used by the tax authority. Rules for participants, 2024. No government payment service on Linkup.
- **Linkup (PDGA).** X-Road federation, pilot since 2025; members PNEA, PLR and PNIA; onboarding procedure documented; MoEYS not a member. The interoperability framework of 2021 is published but not applied.
- **PLR (learner registry).** Enrolment records for some learners, served over Linkup to PNEA alone. No law names it the learner register. Partner-funded.
- **PEMIS (MoEYS).** Totals by district, grade and sex; file export only. Named in the education act of 2011 as the source of education statistics.

## Reading the output

### 1. The sector systems are sorted out

PLR and PEMIS come out as education's own systems, on reasons drawn straight from the descriptions: some learners only, no law, file export. That matches how the page sorts them, and it stops a ministry from funding either as national infrastructure.

### 2. "X-Road" was read as "open standards"

The description says only that Linkup is an X-Road federation. Marking mark 3 Yes used the assistant's own knowledge of X-Road, which the prompt forbids. The answer is probably right, but by the prompt's own rule it should have been Not known, with a fact to check.

### 3. Mark 2 does not see the missing Payments block

PayPro passes as foundational with gaps, yet no education programme can pay through it: there is no Payments block on Linkup through whose payer bank a programme would reach it. The four marks test the system, not the path a sector takes to it. Check that path before counting PayPro as available.
