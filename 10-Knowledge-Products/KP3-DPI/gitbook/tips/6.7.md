## Reading the output

### 1. The refusal is reasoned from the roadmap itself

CM-05 is proposed "not accepted" because C-15 depends on C-14, which the dependency table places in the second horizon. That reason is in the input, and it is the kind a commenter can accept. Every row names identifiers that exist.

### 2. One proposal changes a validated score

CM-02 proposes lowering I2 and with it the domain score. The run is right on the rule, but changing a score is the team's decision, and it moves the maturity table, the domain report and possibly a gap. Treat "accepted" here as a question for the team, not a change to make.

### 3. Phases 2 to 4 have not run

The matrix is a proposal. Before phase 2, a person decides each row and the three figures are checked at their source. The consistency check of phase 4 must still show that every gap, component and line traces back.

## What next

Once the team has decided each row, phases 2 to 4 revise the documents; compare the result with [E9](../examples/E9_validation-response-matrix.md). The adopted roadmap is then followed month by month, as in [6.8](./6-8.md).
