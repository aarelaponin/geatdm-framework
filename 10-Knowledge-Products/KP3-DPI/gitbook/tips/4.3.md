## Example input

**[the service]:** Progressa learner registration, run by MoEYS. **[country X]:** Progressa.

**[the fields]:**

- Applicant's full name — the parent or learner who signs in; checked against PNIA
- Applicant's date of birth — to confirm the applicant is an adult or a learner of 16 or over
- Learner's given names, family name, date of birth — the learner being registered
- School, grade — where the learner is placed
- Transfers from another school (yes or no), with the transfer letter

**[the authentication methods]:** PNIA offers a PIN or password; a one-time code by SMS; and, at assisted counters, a fingerprint with a one-time code.

  - idbb:acr:biometrics-generated-code   # fingerprint and code

The learner's own fields are typed by the applicant. They are not claims: most learners are below 16 and hold no identity number.

## Reading the output

### 1. Every claim has its field

The two claims map to the two applicant fields and nothing else; address, email and phone are struck because no field needs them. The identifier kept is the service's own subject, as the specification and the learner register's design require.

### 2. Refusing the PIN is a choice, not a rule

The model refused a PIN or password on its own. The specification lets the country and the service choose. It is a defensible level for a register of children, but the owner of the service must decide it, and the assisted counter must then always issue a code.

### 3. The profile scope releases more than two claims

Asking for profile may release claims the form never uses. Check whether PNIA lets a client ask for single claims by name; the specimen marks the same point [confirm]. Then plan the check: an enrolled test person signs in, and a second test service must receive a different subject.

## What next

The identifier kept here is the field the [learner register's schema](../module-3/3-3.md) stores beside its own learner number. The connection is used in the [once-only registration](../module-5/5-3.md) and proved by its [acceptance checks](../module-5/5-4.md).
