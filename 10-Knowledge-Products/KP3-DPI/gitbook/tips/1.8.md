## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa.

**[paste the proposal]:** A Scholarship Management Platform for MoEYS, with the Ministry of Finance. It takes applications from learners and families, checks eligibility, and pays awards. It would hold applicants' names, dates of birth, school, household income and bank details. It includes its own applicant login, its own copy of the list of enrolled learners, and a payment module that sends payment files to the banks. MoEYS would run it.

**[paste the list]:**

- PNIA: national identity and sign-in for people.
- Linkup: the national data exchange, operated by PDGA; members PNEA, PLR, PNIA.
- PayPro: the fast-payment system; no government payments service on Linkup yet.
- PLR: education's learner registry; enrolment records for some learners.

## Example output

**1. Who else would use it?** The eligibility rules and the award workflow are education's. The payment function is not: the Social Protection Agency and the Ministry of Agriculture's input subsidy also pay people, and would need the same route to the banks.

**2. What does it need from the foundation?**

- Identity: PNIA's sign-in, instead of its own login.
- Learners: a read of PLR over Linkup, instead of its own copy of the list.
- Payments: a Payments block on Linkup, reaching PayPro through its payer bank, instead of its own payment files.
- Exchange: Linkup, which MoEYS would have to join.

**3. Who should own it?** MoEYS, whose mandate covers scholarships; it serves one sector. The Payments block it needs serves the whole country and belongs elsewhere.

**Recommended answer:** a sector's application, built on the shared blocks.

**Shared systems the proposal would duplicate:** PNIA's sign-in (its own login); PLR (its copy of the learner list); the shared payment route (its payment module).

## Reading the output

### 1. The duplicates are named from the proposal itself

Each duplicate comes from a line of the proposal: its own login, its own list, its own payment files. That makes the recommendation easy to challenge and hard to wave away.

### 2. "Belongs elsewhere" is not an answer

The run says the Payments block serves the whole country but names no owner, because the list gave none. It also assumed the household income can be read from somewhere; no shared register for it was on the list. Both are guesses the proposal's owner can rightly contest.

### 3. The platform waits for a block that does not exist

No Payments block is on Linkup yet, and MoEYS is not a member. Check when each is planned before the platform is funded, or it will build its own payment module after all.

## What next

The recommendation goes to the body that decides shared infrastructure; [1.9](./1-9.md) then places the platform after the blocks it needs. The cost side of the same choice is weighed in [6.2](../module-6/6-2.md).
