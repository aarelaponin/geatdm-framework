## Example input

**[the register or service]:** PLR and the scholarship pilot, pilot province, October 2028. **[country X]:** Progressa. **[threshold]:** 10 per cent. All figures invented for this example.

**[paste the table]:**

| Indicator | September | October | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Learners in PLR | 21,400 | 23,050 | — |
| Records passing the quality gates | 96.1% | 93.8% | 95% |
| MoEYS calls on Linkup | 12,300 | 18,900 | — |
| Assisted registrations | 1,150 | 1,240 | — |

**[paste] (notes, names and identifiers removed):**

- Load L-10-03: 1,820 in; 1,702 to gold; 118 set aside (duplicate learner 64, school identifier not found 41, date of birth missing 13). Owner: data steward.
- Load L-10-17: 950 in; 905 to gold; 40 set aside (school identifier not found). Owner: data steward.
- Payment batch PB-10: 600 instructed; 588 paid; 9 returned by the payer bank (account closed); 3 with no status. No owner named.

## Example output

**1. What moved**

- Records passing the quality gates: 96.1% to 93.8%, below the 95% target.
- MoEYS calls on Linkup: 12,300 to 18,900.

**2. What failed**

- Load L-10-17: 950 in, 905 to gold and 40 set aside; the counts do not reconcile.
- Rows set aside in October: school identifier not found 81; duplicate learner 64; date of birth missing 13.
- Batch PB-10: 600 instructed, 588 paid, 9 returned (account closed), 3 with no status; the counts do not reconcile.

**3. What needs a decision**

- The fall below the quality target has no named owner. Question for the board: who acts, and by when?
- The 3 payments with no status and the 9 returned have no named owner. Question: who traces them through the payment-status service, and who tells the families?

## Reading the output

### 1. The exceptions are the right ones

The two loads and the batch are checked against their own counts, and the quality figure is reported against its target with both values. Nothing is called resolved. That is supported by the input.

### 2. The prompt forbids the arithmetic it needs

To say that L-10-17 does not reconcile, the run had to add 905 and 40 and compare the sum with 950. It also added the set-aside rows by reason, 41 and 40, into 81. Those are computed figures, which the prompt rules out; show the sum, or have the reconciliation note state the difference.

### 3. The cause of the quality fall is not in the tables

Most set-aside rows fail on the school identifier, which points at the identifier mapping of wave 1. That is a lead, not a finding. Ask the data steward before the board reads it as the cause.

## What next

The exceptions go to the joint board set up in [6.6](./6-6.md); each stays open until its owner records what was done and a later reconciliation shows it. The yearly review against the safeguards draws on these reports, as [E7](../examples/E7_roadmap.md) sets out in its monitoring tiers.
