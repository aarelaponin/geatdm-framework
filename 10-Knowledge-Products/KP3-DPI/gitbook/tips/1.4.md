## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa. **[education]:** education.

**QUESTIONS:**

- DAT-Q03 (D2): Is there an authoritative register of learners, and which body keeps it? Good: one register kept by a named body under law, linked to the national identity.
- DAT-Q04 (D6): At what level does the education information system hold data? Good: learner-level records, totals derived from them.
- INT-Q01 (I2): Is there a national data exchange, and is the education ministry a member? Good: a running exchange with the education bodies as members.
- IDN-Q02 (N3): Do education services check a person through the national identity authority's sign-in? Good: every education service that needs identity does.

**SOURCES:** MoEYS education statistics yearbook 2025; PNIA annual report 2025; PDGA note on the Linkup pilot, 2025; education act of 2011.

## Reading the output

### 1. DAT-Q03 rests on its quotation

The finding on the learner register says only what the yearbook and the act show, and its stage, Basic, matches the good answer's distance. It agrees with desk result AF-DAT-01.

### 2. DAT-Q04 is wrong, and the confidence says so

One table heading was read as learner-level data and given Systematic. The 0.4 is the warning: verification later showed PEMIS holds district totals only (E4). The low confidence did its job by putting this point first.

### 3. IDN-Q02 should partly be NO SOURCE

The sources say who calls PNIA's read on Linkup; none says anything about the sign-in. "No education service is shown using it" turns silence into a finding. Ask PNIA for its list of sign-in clients.
