## Example input

**[the identity authority]:** the Progressa National Identity Authority (PNIA). **[country X]:** Progressa.

**[its service description]:** a summary of PNIA's published service description, version of 2024:

- **e-KYC sign-in.** GET /authorize, POST /token, GET /userinfo, and GET /keys for the signing keys. A service becomes a client by a signed form sent to PNIA, which issues the client identifier by hand. The identifier of the person in the token is not stated.
- **Person read on Linkup.** GET /person/{nationalNumber} returns name, date of birth and status. Server to server, no person present. Callable by PNEA only.
- **e-KYC match.** POST /ekyc/verify takes a national number and a one-time code or a face photo, and returns match or no match. For banks and mobile operators.
- Enrolment and card replacement happen only at PNIA offices.

- GET /person/{nationalNumber}: no person present; keyed on the national number.
- POST /ekyc/verify: person present (code or photo); keyed on the national number.

**For the steering committee.** PNIA's sign-in is close to the published verification interface but is not it: no discovery address, no client registration by interface. Its two reads keyed on the national number are Progressa's own contracts, not GovStack's.

## Reading the output

### 1. The two own contracts are named as PNIA's

The table keeps the read by national number apart from the published set and says it is PNIA's, as the safeguard asks. That reading is in the sources: PNEA alone calls it, server to server.

### 2. The sign-in description is invented

Progressa's sources say PNIA runs a sign-in, not what its interface looks like. The paths, the paper form and the e-KYC match in the input were made up for this run. A real description, read against the build plan's settled choice, replaces them.

### 3. The subject identifier must be checked first

The description does not say what identifier the token carries. If it is the national number, every client would receive it, which the published block forbids. Ask PNIA before anything else; the model rightly could not answer it.
