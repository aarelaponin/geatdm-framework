## Reading the output

### 1. "No mandate found" lands where the assessment found gaps

The run left PLR without a mandate, as the education act names no learner register (F-GOV-1). It did the same for the board, which no text creates yet. Both are earned, and both point to the amendment and the cabinet decision drafted in the first wave.

### 2. The terms say "decides" where the board can only recommend

The draft terms give the board decisions that no mandate supports. Until a cabinet decision or a ministerial order sets it up, it can recommend. The run also read "operates" as a power to set Linkup's rules for other ministries, which PDGA's coordinating mandate may not carry.

### 3. Two bodies are thin in the draft

The Data Protection Commission appears only as an observer, though the rules for children's data rest on its act. Check too whether the ICT Steering Committee, which met twice in 2024, has a text that could be revived instead of a new board.
