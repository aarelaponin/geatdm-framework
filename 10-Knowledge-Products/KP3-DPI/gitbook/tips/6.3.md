## Reading the output

### 1. The order holds against the table

Every component sits at or after the wave of what it waits for, and the three pairs that share a wave are named. That check is mechanical and supported by the input.

### 2. The run used start rules the prompt does not ask for

By dependencies alone, C-05 could open in W1 and C-11 in W2. The draft keeps them in W2 and W4 only because the pasted list carried a filing month and a "not before" note. Without those notes the prompt gives a different plan; the list should say why each component waits.

### 3. "Drafted" and "in force" are two states of one component

The table can check that C-02 is drafted by W1. It cannot check when the amendment is in force, which the national rollout needs. Track that date with the cabinet's calendar, and check each wave against the year its money can arrive.
