## Reading the output

### 1. The foundational blocks are the part that truly moves

PNIA, Linkup and the Payments block are national, so health connects to them instead of building them. That is supported by the input and by how the blocks are owned. The cost argument of the education case carries over with them.

### 2. "Unchanged" is a proposal, and one row may be wrong

The nine steps carry over, but the questionnaires name education bodies and school registers, so their sessions and respondents change. The run also listed the Ministry of Finance as a body that stays; whether it runs anything for health is not in the input. Test each "unchanged" row in the health assessment.

### 3. A lock-in question the education run never met

The health pilot runs on a funded contract, and the list does not say who holds its maintenance. Check that contract against the four lock-in checks before the patient index is planned.
