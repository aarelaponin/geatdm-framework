## Reading the output

### 1. The headings and levels survive

Every heading is quoted as pasted, including the specification's own odd punctuation in 6.3.3.2, and the one RECOMMENDED stays RECOMMENDED. That is the safeguard's point, and it holds in this run; the line-by-line check is still needed on the full 42.

### 2. A compliance report is not a test result

The run offered the vendor "your GovStack compliance report" as evidence of an operation. That overreaches. GovStack's listing is a review of what the provider submitted, graded Level 1 or 2; it is not a run of the operation. Strike that option and ask for the test run itself.

### 3. What the questionnaire still lacks

It asks nothing on how a service description is created or changed, which no published operation covers, and nothing on which edition the vendor's answers refer to. Add both, and check whether GovStack lists the product and at which level.

## What next

The answers let you choose the product, and the product's named format is what [2.3](./2-3.md) needs to draft the service description. The [build plan](../build-pack.md) records the choice as one of its three settings.
