## Reading the output

### 1. Every check has a row that must fail it

Each check carries one invented test row, and the three seeded faults of the tier specimen are among them: a duplicate, an empty date of birth, and a child of three in grade six. The messages are plain sentences a head teacher can act on.

### 2. Everything is critical

The run made all six checks critical. That was a judgment, not the owner's note. A strict rule on a misspelt status sets aside rows a person at staging could fix, and the share of failed rows will look worse than the data is.

### 3. The commonest duplicate is not caught

The duplicate check runs on the learner number, but a school's new learners have none. A child sent twice by two schools passes. The owner needs a matching rule on names, date of birth and school, and a test row for it.

## What next
