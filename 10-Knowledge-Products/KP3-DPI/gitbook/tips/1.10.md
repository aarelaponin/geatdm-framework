## Reading the output

### 1. Identity is rightly reused

The service signs parents in through PNIA, on evidence quoted from the table. Building a separate identity for learners would duplicate what runs.

### 2. The editions did not come from the input

Nothing in the maturity table names a specification or its edition. This run took them from the course's sources; a run without them would invent editions that look exact. Check each against the GovStack specification site.

### 3. "Exists" needs the findings behind it

PNIA's sign-in runs, but no education service is its client, and children below 16 have no identity number. The shortlist needs a provisional learner identifier. Check every "Yes" against the verified findings before it reaches the minister.
