## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa.

**[the payment landscape]:** the government's accounts are held by the Ministry of Finance treasury at the central bank. PayPro, the national fast-payment system, is operated by the central bank and used by the tax authority. Five commercial banks take part in PayPro. Two mobile money providers serve most rural households; neither is yet on PayPro. There is no aggregator.

**[the programmes]:**

- Scholarships (MoEYS with the Ministry of Finance): paid by cheque from the MoEYS account at the treasury.
- A social grant programme (Social Protection Agency): paid in cash at district offices, from its own treasury account.

## Example output

| Block component | What already fills it in Progressa | What is missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account mapper | Nothing | A mapper from learner or beneficiary number to account |
| Payment request initiation | Cheque runs at MoEYS; cash lists at the agency | Batches sent by a service |
| Payment gateway | Nothing | One gateway for both programmes |
| Payment portal and vouchers | Nothing | A portal for officers; vouchers not needed yet |
| Reconciliation | Manual, against bank statements | Status per payment, matched to each batch |
| Payer bank behind the block | Treasury accounts at the central bank | A named payer bank holding each programme's account |

Settlement between banks, and the identity and know-your-customer checks on account holders, stay with the central bank, the banks and the mobile money providers. They are not the block's.

**Payment systems the block would connect to, through the payer bank**

- PayPro, the national fast-payment system
- The five commercial banks, as PayPro participants
- The two mobile money providers, once they are reachable through PayPro or the payer bank

## Reading the output

### 1. Nothing is assigned outside the block's role

The table keeps settlement and the customer checks with the financial institutions, and PayPro stays behind the payer bank rather than inside the block. That is what the specification and the build plan say.

### 2. Much of the landscape is invented

The sources say only that PayPro is run by the central bank and used by the tax authority, and that scholarships are paid by cheque. The five banks, the two mobile money providers, the treasury account and the cash grants were made up for this run, so the systems list is an example, not Progressa's market.

### 3. The mobile money route must be checked

Rural families are reached mostly by mobile money, and the table assumes a route through PayPro that does not exist yet. Ask the central bank whether mobile money providers can join PayPro, and which bank will be the payer bank.
