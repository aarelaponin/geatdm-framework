## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa.

**[paste the steps]**, learner registration as MoEYS describes it:

1. The parent and learner sign in with the national identity.
2. The form fills with the name and date of birth released.
3. The parent types the school, the grade and a contact number.
4. The parent submits; the checks run.
5. The registrar approves.
6. The record is written to the learner register.
7. The service confirms the record exists.
8. The parent receives a text message confirming the registration.

**[paste them]**, the published operations:

- Identity 2.0 (PNIA): the sign-in on PNIA's own page; `POST /oauth/token`; `GET /oidc/userinfo`.
- Digital Registries 3.0-alpha (PLR): `POST /data/{registryName}/{versionNumber}/updateOrCreate`; `POST /data/{registryName}/{versionNumber}/exists`.

## Example output

**The block that holds the order of the steps:** the registration service.

1. **Step 1.** The browser carries the person to PNIA's sign-in page. Provider: PNIA, Identity 2.0. Operation: the sign-in (no operation name in the pasted list). Does not cross the exchange.
2. **Step 2.** Registration service → PNIA: `POST /oauth/token`, then `GET /oidc/userinfo`; the service places the two claims into the form. Does not cross the exchange.
3. **Steps 3 to 5.** Inside the registration service; no call to another block.
4. **Step 6.** Registration service → PLR, Digital Registries: `POST /data/{registryName}/{versionNumber}/updateOrCreate`. Crosses Linkup: carried through the caller's own security server, grant checked, signed, time-stamped and logged.
5. **Step 7.** Registration service → PLR: `POST /data/{registryName}/{versionNumber}/exists`. Crosses Linkup, as call 4.

**Steps with no published operation**

- Step 8, the text message: NO PUBLISHED OPERATION.

## Reading the output

### 1. Earned: the sequence sits in the right block

The list puts the order in the registration service and keeps the exchange to carrying, checking, signing and logging. Every operation quoted for steps 2, 6 and 7 is in the pasted list, word for word.

### 2. Overstated: call 1 has no exact operation

The prompt asks for the operation "quoted exactly". For the sign-in it could not do that, because the pasted list names a page, not an endpoint, and the run said so instead of inventing one. A reviewer should still fetch PNIA's `/.well-known/openid-configuration` to name it.

### 3. Missing: who sends the text message

Step 8 is a real need of the owner. The Messaging block is cited in this course but not built, so the step is a question for MoEYS and PDGA, not a design decision. Note too that no grant is listed for the two calls to PLR yet.
