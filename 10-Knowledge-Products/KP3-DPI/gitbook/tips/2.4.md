## Reading the output

### 1. One failure for each rule

A1 to A4 agree with Progressa's specimen test applications, and the run added A5 and A6 so that the grade rule and the file rule each have a failing test of their own. Each fails on one rule alone, which is what makes a failure readable.

### 2. A2 depends on a person who does not exist yet

The name comparison can only be tested with a test person enrolled with PNIA, who signs in and approves sharing `sub` and `name`. That person is still a placeholder. The run wrote A2 as if it could be sent today; it cannot, and nothing here has run.

### 3. Check the age wording and the grade range

The specimen's rule reads "older than age 3" while its message says "at least three": a learner exactly three on 1 September passes one and fails the other. And the register's schema accepts grades 1 to 12, not 13. Take both from the education act before the rules are set.
