## Example output

**Screens and fields**

- A required field, sex, is added to the applicant form. The parent must now answer one more question. **Touches the write**: it fills a field the register requires.

**Rules and checks**

- Grade now runs 1 to 12, not 13. A parent can no longer choose grade 13. **Touches the write**: it matches the register's range.
- The transfer letter may also be a PNG. A parent with a phone photo no longer has to convert it.

**Process flow**

- A new human role, the head teacher's confirmation, comes before the registrar. Every file now waits for the head teacher, who may approve, reject or send back. **Touches who decides.**

**Service settings**

- The confirmation is issued in English and the national language. The parent receives it in two languages.

## Reading the output

### 1. Each change is said in the owner's terms

Every difference names what it changes for the parent or the officer, under the four headings, and the three that touch the decision or the write are marked. That is earned from the two files.

### 2. The new role needs more than a mark

The run marked the head teacher's role but did not say that a head teacher may now reject a file the law gives to the registrar. Whether a head teacher may decide at all is a question for the legal text, not for the note. The language setting is also a guess: it may belong to one installation and not travel with the file.

### 3. Check what the export left out

The note compares what is in the two files. It cannot see a setting the product did not export. Import file B into a clean test installation and compare it screen by screen with the original before the note is sent. Nothing here has run.

## What next

The note goes to the owner of the service, and the question of who decides goes back to the deciding office mapped in [2.1](./2-1.md). The register the service writes to is set up in [Module 3](../module-3/3-1.md).
