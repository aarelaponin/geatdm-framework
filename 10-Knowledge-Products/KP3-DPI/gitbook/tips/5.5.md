## Reading the output

### 1. Earned: counts and faults come only from the records

The table and the failures use the fields in the export and nothing else. The report does not say what was written to the register, because monitoring holds no message content.

### 2. Overstated: "first appearance"

The prompt asks what appears for the first time in the period, but an export of one week cannot show that. The report can only say "first in this export"; it needs the previous period's export to say more.

### 3. Check next: one failure is planned

The D4 11:40 refusal to PNEA looks like the deliberate second call of check X3, not a fault, while the D3 refusal to the registration service is a real failure. Match each failure to the sheet of checks before reporting it. The export holds SS-PLR's records only, so it says nothing about calls between other members.
