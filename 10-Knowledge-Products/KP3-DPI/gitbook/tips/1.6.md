## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa.

**[source A]:** desk result AF-DAT-02, drawn from the MoEYS education statistics yearbook 2025, table 3.2: the yearbook appears to report learner-level data for secondary schools. Dated 2025.

**[source B]:** questionnaire answer 2.2 from the MoEYS statistics unit, signed by the head of unit, week 6: PEMIS holds district totals only; some secondary schools keep learner lists in spreadsheets. Attached: the blank school return and the PEMIS data dictionary.

**[the point]:** whether PEMIS, the education information system, holds a record for each learner.

## Example output

| | Source A | Source B |
|---|---|---|
| **What it says** | PEMIS appears to hold learner-level data for secondary schools | PEMIS holds totals by district, grade and sex; learner lists sit in school spreadsheets |
| **Where from** | Statistics yearbook 2025, table 3.2, read by the desk assessment | Signed answer of the statistics unit, with the return form and data dictionary |
| **Level on the ladder** | 3, official statistics, as read by the desk assessment | 4, the unit's own account with internal documents |

**Evidence that would settle the point:** level 1, PEMIS shown working in a session: its list of data tables, and the query that produces yearbook table 3.2.

**Question for the clarification interview:** "Can you take us through how yearbook table 3.2 is produced, from what reaches PEMIS to the published figure?"

**Ask the section lead to bring:** PEMIS open on screen with its data tables listed; the worksheet behind table 3.2; one secondary school's return for 2025.

## Reading the output

### 1. The settling evidence is the right one

Asking to see PEMIS and the query behind the table goes to level 1, which outranks both sources. That is what session V-01 did, and it corrected the desk result (E4).

### 2. The ladder alone would have picked the wrong side

By level, the yearbook (3) beats the unit's answer (4). Yet the yearbook was not wrong; the desk assessment misread it. The level belongs to the yearbook, not to a reading of it. The ladder has no row for a questionnaire answer, so placing it at 4 is a judgement to state.

### 3. Record the reason, not just the result

The facilitator decides which source stands and writes why in the reconciled-response table. Here: "corrected", PEMIS shown in V-01, level 1. That record lets finding F-DAT-2 be traced back.
