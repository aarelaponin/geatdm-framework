## Reading the output

### 1. No licence was named from memory

The run quoted no licence because none was pasted, and said plainly that copying is not permitted. That is the safeguard working: the note rests on the empty licence field, not on a guess about what UNICEF would allow.

### 2. "May follow" is stated more firmly than it should be

Part 1 treats following the design as free. That is the usual reading, and the subtopic takes it, but it is a legal judgment the run cannot make. The second question for the lawyer is the right place for it; part 1 should point to it.

### 3. Check the other repository and keep the commit

The note covers one repository at one commit. The subtopic names two public Giga repositories; check the second before anything from it is used, and keep the commit in every citation, because the documentation can change.
