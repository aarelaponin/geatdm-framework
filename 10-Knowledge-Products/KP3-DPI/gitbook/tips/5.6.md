## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa.

**[paste them]**, the services Linkup would list once configurations X1 to X7 are built (drafted from the specimens; no export exists yet):

- PLR `enrolment-api`: a read of enrolment.
- PLR `plr-register`: `updateOrCreate`, `exists` (Digital Registries 3.0-alpha).

## Reading the output

### 1. Earned: no learner list, no payment link

The note shows the team it can confirm a learner through `exists` and pay through the Payments block, which is what the page says the foundation now offers. Asking for `exists` alone keeps the grant narrow.

### 2. Risky: the list is a plan, not an export

The services were drafted from the specimens, because nothing is built. The safeguard asks for the exchange's own list. The note must not go to the team until the list is exported and each owner has confirmed its conditions, and the Payments block's owner is still unnamed.

### 3. Missing: PNIA's sign-in

The prompt asks only about registered services, so PNIA's sign-in, which does not cross the exchange, is left out. Yet it is the published way to confirm an applicant. Add it by hand, marked as outside the list, before the note goes out.
