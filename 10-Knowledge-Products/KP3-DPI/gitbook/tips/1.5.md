## Reading the output

### 1. The totals are well supported

The blank return form shows schools sending totals, which backs the claim that PEMIS holds no learner records. That is the point the desk assessment got wrong, and the questionnaire already holds the evidence to correct it.

### 2. A passage cannot prove an absence

The run marked "the act names no learner register" as unsupported because only one section was pasted. That is right, but it also means the run is only as good as the passages pasted. Ask for the whole act, not an extract.

### 3. PNEA's half is thin

The answers speak mostly for MoEYS. PNEA appears only in its matching rule. Before the session, ask PNEA's focal point to confirm the candidate records and the school codes it uses.
