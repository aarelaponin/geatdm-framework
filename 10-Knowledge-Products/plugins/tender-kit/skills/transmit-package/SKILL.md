# Transmit Package

## What this produces

The cover with which the buying unit — the unit that prepared the package — sends a finished tender package to the authority that approves and issues it (and on to the funder): a **transmittal memorandum** (1–3 pages) and a **short cover email**. The memo is also the **decision record** — it is where the procurement-vehicle choice and the open decisions are documented so the choice is defensible and nothing is lost.

Write it in the buying unit's voice. Where an adviser helped prepare the package, the memo may say so in one line; the unit still owns the recommendation, and the authority decides.

## The transmittal memo — structure

1. **Header block** — To (the head of the contracting authority and its tender or procurement committee) / Cc (the funder's task team) / From (the head of the buying unit, or its project lead for the unit) / Date / Subject / classification (Draft for review).
2. **Purpose** — the buying unit transmits the draft package for the committee's review and approval, for completion in the funder's standard document and submission for the funder's no-objection.
3. **Documents transmitted** — a short table of the files, noting any that **supersede** an earlier draft (so the old one is withdrawn).
4. **Recommended procurement vehicle** — the unit's recommendation (e.g. supply/IS RFP with rated criteria) with a two-to-three-sentence rationale, **and the option considered and not recommended** (e.g. consulting QCBS — obligation of means) with its one-line reason. This is the decision trail.
5. **Design choices already settled** — the things the committee doesn't need to re-litigate: scope boundary, single turnkey + advisory/capacity as deliverables, independent IV&V retained separately, self-contained requirements, result-obligation machinery, local-capability approach, funding-window schedule.
6. **Decisions for the authority to close before issuance** — the open `[confirm]` items: Data Sheet values, annex/legal-text finalisation, appointing the IV&V agent, pilot-agency commitments, hosting, post-funding sustainability, and completing the funder's bidding-document wrapper for no-objection.
7. **Time sensitivity** — the funding-window/closing-date reality and the critical-path message (launch and pre-clear with the funder now).
8. **Requested action** — a short numbered list (review and confirm the vehicle; close the open decisions or authorise the defaults; authorise the buying unit to complete the funder's wrapper and seek the no-objection), with a sign-off block for the head of the unit.

## The cover email — keep it short

Route the memo; don't repeat it. One short paragraph on the recommendation, one on the timing, the attachment list, and an offer to walk the committee through it. Use bracketed placeholders for recipients/names. Note that attachments are added manually (a drafting tool can't attach the files).

## Worked example — Progressa

*Simulated case: Progressa is the fictional country of the courses; the case is in `../../references/progressa-tender-case.md`. Posts, never names.*

```
To:       Chair, Tender Committee, Ministry of ICT
Cc:       Task team leader, the funder of the National Learner Registry programme
From:     Director-General, PDGA (prepared by PDGA's Linkup project team)
Date:     [date]
Subject:  Linkup, Lot 1 — draft request for proposals and annexes, for review and approval
Status:   Draft for review

Requested action
1. Confirm the vehicle: supply and installation of an information system, rated criteria,
   award to the Most Advantageous Proposal. (Considered and not recommended: a consulting
   Terms of Reference — an obligation of means, without acceptance or warranty.)
2. Close the open decisions listed in section 6, or authorise PDGA to use the defaults marked
   [confirm] in the Bid Data Sheet.
3. Authorise PDGA to complete the funder's standard document and to seek the funder's
   no-objection, so that the lot finishes inside the programme's three years.
```

## Doctrine to preserve

- **Document the decision; don't ship the reject.** Record that the alternative vehicle was considered and why it was rejected — do **not** attach a stale, contradictory alternative document. Offer a one-page options comparison only if the committee formally wants it.
- **Make the open decisions explicit.** The authority should see exactly what it must confirm; never bury policy choices.
- **Flag superseded files.** Tell the committee which earlier draft to withdraw, to avoid version confusion.
- **Lead with the recommendation, not the history.** The memo is for a decision, not a narrative.
