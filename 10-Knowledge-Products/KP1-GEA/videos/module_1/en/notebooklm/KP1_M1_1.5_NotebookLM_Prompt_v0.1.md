# NotebookLM setup for KP1 · M1 · Video 1.5

## Step 1 — Fix the notebook, not just the prompt

NotebookLM weights **sources** far more heavily than the customization box. Takes drift when the
notebook contains the full PAERA / EIF corpus, because the hosts are free to roam it.

1. Create a **new notebook used only for video 1.5**. One notebook per video — never one
   notebook per module.
2. Add `KP1_M1_1.5_AudioBrief_v0.1.md` as the **first** source.
3. Add **at most two** background sources, and only narrow extracts — not the whole document.
   If you can, add none: the brief is self-contained.
4. Deselect every other source before hitting Generate.

## Step 2 — Settings

- Format: **Deep Dive** (two hosts)
- Length: **Shorter** — Default overshoots a 4-minute spec by 60–90 seconds
- Language: English

## Step 3 — Customization prompt (paste this)

```
Follow the source titled "AUDIO BRIEF — KP1 Module 1 Video 1.5" exactly. It is the sole
authority. Do not add anything not in it.

Audience: government Chief Digital Officers, Directors-General and sector ministers, many
listening in English as a second language. Register: two senior advisers briefing a minister —
collegial, precise, unhurried. Not a podcast. Factual, never promotional — PAERA's assets are
stated as facts, not sold. Speak at roughly 140 words per minute.

The listener is the government official who runs these systems, not a citizen being served.
Say "your team", "your ministry", "your minister". Never open with, or return to, the listener
queuing or filling in a form.

Total runtime 4 minutes, hard ceiling 4:15. Follow the brief's eight segments in order and
respect its per-segment time budget: title 15s, the next question 15s, two paths 40s, five
things 75s, every sector reuses 30s, the working network 25s, one sentence 30s, sources 10s.
Finish each segment on a complete sentence before moving to the next, and leave a clear pause
between segments. Number the five things aloud — "first" to "fifth" — with roughly equal time
each; they are the substantive payload of the video.

Never say: "deep dive", "welcome to", "today we're unpacking", "our sources", "the sources say",
"here's where it gets interesting". Do not end with a reflective question, and do not invite the
listener to think about other sectors or institutions. The audio ends on the brief's series
handoff — the lifecycle on one page, the next video — then one line: "Sources are in the video
description." Read no URLs.

Cut all filler — "you know", "like", "I mean", "basically", "totally", "right?", "wow", "oh
absolutely". No crosstalk or interrupting; one speaker finishes before the other begins. The
only permitted expression is "head start", used at most twice; no other metaphor or idiom.

Say "PAERA" (spelled P-A-E-R-A on first mention), "published in 2024 under GovStack", "twelve
to eighteen months" for the own-framework path, "GovMarket", and "register" not "registry".
Invent no other figures, dates, countries or examples.
```

## Step 4 — Fallback if the box truncates

Some NotebookLM builds cut the customization field short. If so, paste only this and rely on the
brief source to carry the detail:

```
Follow the source "AUDIO BRIEF — KP1 Module 1 Video 1.5" exactly, including its 4-minute
runtime and its eight timed segments. Two senior policy advisers briefing a government
minister — not a podcast, and never promotional. The listener is the official who runs these
systems, never a citizen being served. No filler, no crosstalk, no invented examples; the only
permitted expression is "head start". Number the five things aloud, first to fifth. Do not end
with a reflective question; end on the series handoff and one line pointing to sources in the
description.
```

## Step 5 — Check before you accept the take

Download the transcript as `.srt`, then:

```bash
python3 scripts/srt_drift_check.py KP1_M1_1.5_Audio_v0.1.srt --target 240
```

Plus the judgement calls the script cannot make:

- [ ] Runtime 3:50–4:10
- [ ] Opens on the topic, not on a citizen's experience
- [ ] Two paths presented in order, own-framework first
- [ ] "First … fifth" audible on the five things, roughly equal time each
- [ ] Says "PAERA", spelled P-A-E-R-A once; "published in 2024 under GovStack"
- [ ] Factual register on the network slide — no sales language
- [ ] Ends on the handoff into 1.6 — no reflective outro
- [ ] Nothing said that is not in the brief
- [ ] Eight clean segment boundaries you can cut cues against

**If two or more checks fail, regenerate rather than edit.** NotebookLM output is cheaper to
re-roll than to patch, and re-rolls converge once the notebook holds only the brief. If the same
failure survives three re-rolls, the fix belongs in the **brief**, not the prompt.
