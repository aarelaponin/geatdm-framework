# NotebookLM setup for KP1 · M1 · Video 1.8

## Step 1 — Fix the notebook, not just the prompt

NotebookLM weights **sources** far more heavily than the customization box. Takes drift when the
notebook contains the full PAERA / EIF corpus, because the hosts are free to roam it.

1. Create a **new notebook used only for video 1.8**. One notebook per video — never one
   notebook per module.
2. Add `KP1_M1_1.8_AudioBrief_v0.1.md` as the **first** source.
3. Add **at most two** background sources, and only narrow extracts — not the whole document.
   If you can, add none: the brief is self-contained. For this video especially, add nothing
   about the four countries — outside material invites invented statistics.
4. Deselect every other source before hitting Generate.

## Step 2 — Settings

- Format: **Deep Dive** (two hosts)
- Length: **Shorter** — Default overshoots a 4-to-5-minute spec by 60–90 seconds
- Language: English

## Step 3 — Customization prompt (paste this)

```
Follow the source titled "AUDIO BRIEF — KP1 Module 1 Video 1.8" exactly. It is the sole
authority. Do not add anything not in it.

Audience: government Chief Digital Officers, Directors-General and sector ministers, many
listening in English as a second language. Register: two senior advisers briefing a minister —
collegial, precise, unhurried. Not a podcast. Speak at roughly 140 words per minute.

The listener is the government official who runs these systems, not a citizen being served.
Say "your country", "your minister", "your programme". Never open with, or return to, the
listener queuing or filling in a form.

Total runtime 4 minutes 30 seconds, hard ceiling 4:50. Follow the brief's nine segments in
order and respect its per-segment time budget: title 15s, four countries 25s, Rwanda 35s,
Kenya 40s, South Africa 40s, Estonia 45s, the same four elements 30s, one sentence 30s,
sources 10s. Finish each segment on a complete sentence before moving to the next, and leave a
clear pause between segments. Close each country on its stated lesson. Number the four common
elements aloud near the end.

The only countries named are Rwanda, Kenya, South Africa and Estonia — no others, and no
statistics, costs or adoption figures for any of them beyond what the brief states. Kenya is
handled factually: Kenya tried, the obstacles are documented, the debate tells you what to
plan for — no verdict words like "failure" or "cautionary tale". Estonia is the polestar, not
a template to copy.

Never say: "deep dive", "welcome to", "today we're unpacking", "our sources", "the sources say",
"here's where it gets interesting". Do not end with a reflective question, and do not invite the
listener to think about other sectors or institutions. The audio ends on the brief's series
handoff — the rest of the knowledge product, using the fictional country Progressa — then one
line: "Sources are in the video description." Read no URLs.

Cut all filler — "you know", "like", "I mean", "basically", "totally", "right?", "wow", "oh
absolutely". No crosstalk or interrupting; one speaker finishes before the other begins. The
only permitted expression is "polestar", about Estonia only; no other metaphor or idiom.

Pronounce: Irembo "ee-REM-boh"; Huduma "hoo-DOO-mah"; SITA as a word, "SEE-tah"; RIA spelled
R-I-A; X-Road as "X Road". Say "register" not "registry" — "distributed state registers".
```

## Step 4 — Fallback if the box truncates

Some NotebookLM builds cut the customization field short. If so, paste only this and rely on the
brief source to carry the detail:

```
Follow the source "AUDIO BRIEF — KP1 Module 1 Video 1.8" exactly, including its 4-minute-30
runtime and its nine timed segments. Two senior policy advisers briefing a government
minister — not a podcast. Only four countries are named — Rwanda, Kenya, South Africa,
Estonia — with no statistics beyond the brief. Kenya factually, no verdicts; Estonia a
polestar, not a template. Close each country on its lesson and number the four common elements.
No filler, no crosstalk, no invented specifics. Do not end with a reflective question; end on
the series handoff — Progressa — and one line pointing to sources in the description.
```

## Step 5 — Check before you accept the take

Download the transcript as `.srt`, then:

```bash
python3 scripts/srt_drift_check.py KP1_M1_1.8_Audio_v0.1.srt --target 270
```

Plus the judgement calls the script cannot make:

- [ ] Runtime 4:20–4:40
- [ ] Opens on the topic, not on a citizen's experience
- [ ] Countries in deck order — Rwanda, Kenya, South Africa, Estonia — each closing on its lesson
- [ ] No invented statistics, dates or adoption figures for any country
- [ ] Kenya: no verdict language; "the debate tells you what to plan for" framing survives
- [ ] Estonia: "polestar, not a template" stated; says "registers", not "registries"
- [ ] Four common elements numbered aloud
- [ ] Ends on the handoff naming Progressa — no reflective outro
- [ ] Nine clean segment boundaries you can cut cues against

**If two or more checks fail, regenerate rather than edit.** NotebookLM output is cheaper to
re-roll than to patch, and re-rolls converge once the notebook holds only the brief. If the same
failure survives three re-rolls, the fix belongs in the **brief**, not the prompt.
