# NotebookLM setup for KP1 · M1 · Video 1.6

## Step 1 — Fix the notebook, not just the prompt

NotebookLM weights **sources** far more heavily than the customization box. Takes drift when the
notebook contains the full PAERA / EIF corpus, because the hosts are free to roam it.

1. Create a **new notebook used only for video 1.6**. One notebook per video — never one
   notebook per module.
2. Add `KP1_M1_1.6_AudioBrief_v0.3.md` as the **first** source.
3. Add **at most two** background sources, and only narrow extracts — not the whole document.
   If you can, add none: the brief is self-contained.
4. Deselect every other source before hitting Generate.

## Step 2 — Settings

- Format: **Deep Dive** (two hosts)
- Length: **Shorter** — Default overshoots a 4-to-5-minute spec by 60–90 seconds
- Language: English

## Step 3 — Customization prompt (paste this)

```
Follow the source titled "AUDIO BRIEF — KP1 Module 1 Video 1.6" exactly. It is the sole
authority. Do not add anything not in it.

Audience: government Chief Digital Officers, Directors-General and sector ministers, many
listening in English as a second language. Register: two senior advisers briefing a minister —
collegial, precise, unhurried. Not a podcast. Speak at roughly 140 words per minute.

The listener is the government official who runs these systems, not a citizen being served.
Say "your architects", "your minister", "your country". Never open with, or return to, the
listener queuing or filling in a form.

Total runtime 4 minutes 30 seconds, hard ceiling 4:50. Follow the brief's twelve segments in
order and respect its per-segment time budget: title 15s, the one picture 25s, five phases
overview 20s, Discover 30s, Assess 20s, Adapt 30s, Plan 20s, Execute & Govern 25s, four
sign-offs 25s, skipping 20s, week 1 to week 26 30s, sources 10s. Finish each segment on a
complete sentence before moving to the next, and leave a clear pause between segments.

Announce each phase by name and number — "Phase one. Discover." through "Phase five. Execute
and Govern." — and keep the same spoken pattern for each: question, deliverable, sign-off,
duration. Ask the retrieval question early — how long until a roadmap your minister can take to
cabinet, hold that number — leave it unanswered, and answer it explicitly near the end: six
months, week one to week twenty-six.

Never say: "deep dive", "welcome to", "today we're unpacking", "our sources", "the sources say",
"here's where it gets interesting". Do not end with a reflective question, and do not invite the
listener to think about other sectors or institutions. The audio ends on the brief's series
handoff — what you will need from your minister, four asks, the next video — then one line:
"Sources are in the video description." Read no URLs.

Cut all filler — "you know", "like", "I mean", "basically", "totally", "right?", "wow", "oh
absolutely". No crosstalk or interrupting; one speaker finishes before the other begins. Use no
metaphors at all — none is permitted in this video.

Say "PAERA" (spelled P-A-E-R-A on first mention), "register" not "registry", the phases in the
order Discover, Assess, Adapt, Plan, Execute and Govern, and the sourcing question as "build,
buy, share, or sandbox first". Invent no figures, dates, countries or examples beyond the
durations in the brief.

Cover the twelve slides in strict numerical order and finish each before starting the next.
The order is: phase four Plan (slide 7), then the minister's four sign-offs (slide 8), then
phase five Execute and Govern (slide 9), which is the last phase discussed. Never preview a
later slide while an earlier one is being covered.
```

## Step 4 — Fallback if the box truncates

Some NotebookLM builds cut the customization field short. If so, paste only this and rely on the
brief source to carry the detail:

```
Follow the source "AUDIO BRIEF — KP1 Module 1 Video 1.6" exactly, including its 4-minute-30
runtime and its twelve timed segments. Two senior policy advisers briefing a government
minister — not a podcast. The listener is the official who runs these systems, never a citizen
being served. Announce each phase by name and number, "Phase one. Discover." through "Phase
five. Execute and Govern.", each with question, deliverable, sign-off, duration. Ask the
six-months retrieval question early and answer it near the end. No filler, no metaphors, no
crosstalk, no invented examples. Do not end with a reflective question; end on the series
handoff and one line pointing to sources in the description.
```

## Step 5 — Check before you accept the take

Download the transcript as `.srt`, then:

```bash
python3 scripts/srt_drift_check.py KP1_M1_1.6_Audio_v0.1.srt --target 270
```

Plus the judgement calls the script cannot make:

- [ ] Runtime 4:20–4:40
- [ ] Opens on the topic, not on a citizen's experience
- [ ] Retrieval question asked early, left open, answered "six months" near the end
- [ ] All five phases announced by name and number, in order, same pattern each
- [ ] Phase durations correct: 3–4 wks, 6–8 wks, 4–6 wks, 6–8 wks, ongoing
- [ ] "Four sign-offs in six months" and the minister-reviews-four-times point land
- [ ] Ends on the handoff into 1.7 — no reflective outro
- [ ] Nothing said that is not in the brief
- [ ] Twelve clean segment boundaries you can cut cues against

**If two or more checks fail, regenerate rather than edit.** NotebookLM output is cheaper to
re-roll than to patch, and re-rolls converge once the notebook holds only the brief. If the same
failure survives three re-rolls, the fix belongs in the **brief**, not the prompt.
