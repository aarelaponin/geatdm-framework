# NotebookLM setup for KP1 · M1 · Video 1.1

Generated for deck `KP1_M1_1.1_Deck_v0.2.pptx` (v0.3 script, seven-video Module 1).

## Step 1 — Fix the notebook, not just the prompt

NotebookLM weights **sources** far more heavily than the customization box. Takes drift when the
notebook holds the wider PAERA / EIF corpus and the hosts are free to roam it.

Do this before generating:

1. Use the notebook reserved for video 1.1 (`notebooks.json`), or create one used only for 1.1.
2. **Reset its sources.** Remove every earlier brief — the v0.2 deck rewrote this video and an
   older brief left in the notebook will be blended into the take.
3. Add `KP1_M1_1.1_AudioBrief_v0.4.md` as the **only** source. The brief is self-contained;
   adding background documents is what produced the first round of invented specifics.

## Step 2 — Settings

- Format: **Deep Dive** (two hosts)
- Length: **Shorter** — Default overshoots a 4 minutes spec by 60–90 seconds regardless of the prompt
- Language: English

## Step 3 — Customization prompt

```
Follow the source titled "AUDIO BRIEF — KP1 Module 1 Video 1.1" exactly. It is the sole
authority. Do not add anything not in it.

Audience: government Chief Digital Officers, Directors-General and sector ministers, many
listening in English as a second language. Register: two senior advisers briefing a minister —
collegial, precise, unhurried. Not a podcast.

The listener is the government official who runs these systems, not a citizen at a counter.
Say "in your ministry", "your programme", "your vendor", "your minister". Never open with, or
return to, the listener queuing or filling in a form.

Total runtime 4 minutes, hard ceiling 4:15, about 310 spoken words in the whole take.
Follow the brief's 6 segments in order and respect its per-segment budget: title 15s, the pattern 40s, four signs 85s, four costs 45s, root cause 45s, sources 10s.
Finish each segment on a complete sentence before moving to the next. Number the four signs aloud ("sign one" … "sign four") and name the four costs aloud (money, time, citizens, your minister) in the brief's order, minister last and hardest.

Say the brief's bullets and stop. Each bullet is the complete content for its beat, not a topic to
illustrate. Do not add a second example, do not restate a point in other words, do not gloss a
bullet with "so what this means is". If a segment's words run out before its seconds do, leave the
silence — do not fill it.

The last content slide is the recap. Say its single message once and stop — no summary of the
video's sections, no invitation to act, no "before the next video", no mention of a prompt, of
companion material, or of the listener's own sector. That call to action is printed on the slide
and is deliberately not spoken. Then one line: "Sources are in the video description." Read no
URLs, name no section numbers. Those are the final words.

Never say: "deep dive", "welcome to", "today we're unpacking", "our sources", "the sources say",
"here's where it gets interesting". Do not end with a reflective question.

Cut all filler — "you know", "like", "I mean", "basically", "totally", "right?", "wow", "oh
absolutely". No backchannel: while one host speaks the other is silent, no "right", "exactly",
"mm-hmm". No crosstalk; one speaker finishes before the other begins. Use no metaphors except the single one permitted in the brief's root-cause segment — no plumbing, contractors, server rooms or tangled wires.

Say "PAERA" (spelled P-A-E-R-A on first mention), "the European Interoperability Framework", "the once-only principle", "register" not "registry". Invent no figures, dates, countries or examples.
```

## Step 4 — Fallback if the box truncates

Some NotebookLM builds cut the customization field short. If so, paste only this, and rely on
the brief source to carry the detail:

```
Follow the source "AUDIO BRIEF — KP1 Module 1 Video 1.1" exactly, including its 4 minutes
runtime and its 6 timed segments. Two senior policy advisers briefing a government
minister — not a podcast. The listener is the official who runs these systems, never a citizen at
a counter. No filler, no backchannel, no metaphors, no crosstalk, no invented examples. End on the
recap slide's single sentence, then one line pointing to sources in the description — no handoff,
no reflective question, and say nothing about a prompt or the listener's own sector.
```

## Step 5 — Check before you accept the take

- [ ] Runtime 3:50–4:10
- [ ] Opens on the topic, not on a counter queue
- [ ] "Sign one … sign four" audible and in deck order
- [ ] Money / time / citizens / minister named, minister last
- [ ] Says "PAERA", not "PRA"; "once-only", not "ask once"
- [ ] Ends on the recap sentence plus the sources line — no handoff, no reflective outro
- [ ] Nothing said about the practice box: no "prompt", no "companion material", no "your own sector"
- [ ] 6 clean segment boundaries you can cut cues against
