## Step 1 — Fix the notebook, not just the prompt

NotebookLM weights **sources** far more heavily than the customization box. Measured on this
module: a take generated from a 310-word brief invented *more* than one from a 535-word brief
(8 banned phrases against 5). Thin sources are what produce improvisation, so the brief now
carries the deck's full approved substance and it must be the only thing in the notebook.

1. Use the notebook reserved for video 1.7 (`notebooks.json`), or create one used only for it.
2. **Reset its sources.** Remove every earlier brief — an older one left in the notebook gets
   blended into the take.
3. Add `KP1_M1_1.7_AudioBrief_v0.3.md` as the **only** source.

## Step 3 — Customization prompt

```
Follow the source titled "AUDIO BRIEF — KP1 Module 1 Video 1.7" exactly. It is the sole
authority for both what is said and what is not.

This is a policy briefing between two senior advisers preparing a government minister —
collegial, precise, unhurried. Not a podcast. The listener is the government official who runs
these systems, never a citizen at a counter: say "in your ministry", "your programme", "your
minister". Do not open with, or return to, someone queuing or filling in a form.

Work through the brief's section 2 segment by segment, one per slide, in order. In each segment
Host A asks ONE substantive question drawn from that segment's quoted material — the question a
Director-General would ask — and Host B answers using those words, with the slide's bullets as
supporting detail. Finish each segment on a complete sentence before the next.

Everything either host says must be traceable to a line in section 2. If a point would need a
fact, figure, country, institution or example that is not written in the brief, leave the point
out. When a segment's material is covered, move on to the next slide — do not fill the time with
elaboration, a second example or a restatement. A short segment is correct.

Number the asks aloud — "first ask" … "fourth ask" — and name the four governments in the deck's order: Rwanda, Kenya, South Africa, Estonia. Kenya's results are mixed and openly debated; say so.

Total runtime about 5 minutes. Never exceed 6 minutes.

End on the last content slide's message, said once, then one line: "Sources are in the video
description." Read no URLs and name no section numbers. Those are the final words — no reflective
question, no thought to leave the listener with, no mention of other sectors or private
companies, no "before the next video", and nothing about a prompt, companion material or the
listener's own sector.

Never say: "deep dive", "welcome to", "today we're unpacking", "our sources", "the sources say",
"here's where it gets interesting". Never say "broken", "chaos", "nightmare", "crazy", "insane".
Cut all filler — "you know", "like", "I mean", "basically", "right?", "wow". No backchannel:
while one host speaks the other is silent, no "right", "exactly", "mm-hmm". No crosstalk. No
metaphors.

Say "PAERA" (spelled P-A-E-R-A on first mention), "EA Board" exactly, "the once-only principle", "registers" not "registries", and the names Irembo, Huduma Centres, Huduma Namba, SITA (say the letters) and X-Road as written.
```
