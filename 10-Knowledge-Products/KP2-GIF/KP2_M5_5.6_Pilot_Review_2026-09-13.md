## The three answers

**Q1 — does the demo block survive the remix? Yes on order, no on form.** Both takes (v0.2 5:10, v0.4 4:05) reach the seven demo slides in deck order (`coverage_check.py`: IN ORDER, v0.4 at 0:48 → 3:42), and the hand cues land on the hosts' beats. Neither take numbered the observations ("First… Second…"), and both still open on second-person citizen framing ("dealing with government paperwork… you don't actually exist"), carry filler/backchannel, and v0.2 invents three analogies (keys to rooms, a skeleton key, a weather widget). **No take is accepted.** The order fallback (§7: un-park TTS) is not triggered.

**Q2 — do the frames read on a phone? The captions do, the frames do not.** At ~400 px wide the slide title and caption strip read; inside a frame only structure reads — blank vs filled rows, the red "denied" header, green vs red result boxes. The console text itself is ~2 px. At 1080p all of it reads. The 1280×720 @1.5 capture (text 1.5× the 1920 @1 default) was already applied; the next step is the plan's other fix, **crop each frame beat to its card** (C1/C2/C6 the form, C4 the legal pane, C5 the two result boxes) in `beats.yaml`.

**Q3 — is the capture reproducible? Yes.** Two consecutive `scripts/demo-capture.sh` runs: all five frames, the clip's still and all three text captures byte-identical; only the clip MP4 (real-time shot spacing) and `takes.json` (timestamp) differ.

- **The plan's observations, corrected by the live stack:** the tally reads `pre-filled 9 / 9`, not `9 / 10`; the journal banner survives Restore, so C6 ends on Reset.
- `?filming=1` also strips the fault's per-request `detail`, per-call milliseconds and the KP codes from what lands on a slide.
- Versions follow the kit's convention (cues and video carry the **audio** version): `Cues_v0.4`, `Video_v0.4`, not the plan's `v0.1`. The v0.4 audio was padded 6 s at render so Sources holds.
- `draft_cues.py` bunched neighbouring demo slides (3–6, 7–9) against this take; cues are hand-authored. `slide_terms` now reads a demo slide's title and caption only.
- While a clip plays it replaces the whole slide, so slide 8's caption and provenance are off screen for ~20 s.
