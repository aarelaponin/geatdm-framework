**File:** `video/KP2_M2_2.1_Video_v0.2.mp4` · **Subtitles:** `audio/KP2_M2_2.1_Audio_v0.2.srt`

**Title:** The legal layer: why a working interoperability bus carries nothing without a decree

**Description:**

```text
Without the legal layer, the bus cannot lawfully carry one cross-agency message.
The decree is the on-switch, not paperwork.

GIF · Module 2 · 2.1 — Why the platform needs a legal mandate

A flawless technical bus can sit silent for a year, because no official will
share data without a lawful basis — and the most cautious one is right to stop
it. A missing mandate rarely looks like a legal problem. It shows up as a
refusal, a months-long stall waiting on a one-off legal opinion, or a workaround
that is a breach waiting to surface. One whole-of-government decree flips the
default: it makes cross-agency exchange lawful, makes connection the default,
protects the citizen's data, and names the operating authority. The lawful basis
is written once and every agency reuses it.
```

```text
A sound decree is five parts. Knowing them lets you brief a Ministry of Justice

GIF · Module 2 · 2.2 — Anatomy of an interoperability decree

The Explanatory Memorandum makes the case for the minister. The Preamble and the
Draft Articles go to the lawyer: the authority and the binding rules. The Cover
```

### 2.4 — The Draft Articles Package

**File:** `video/KP2_M2_2.4_Video_v0.6.mp4` · **Subtitles:** `audio/KP2_M2_2.4_Audio_v0.6.srt`

**Title:** Drafting the binding articles of an interoperability decree — without inventing law

**Description:**

```text
The operative articles — mandatory connection, once-only, data protection —
generated against published models, every clause confirmed, not invented.

GIF · Module 2 · 2.4 — The Draft Articles Package

The articles do three jobs: they force participation (scope and mandatory
connection), protect the citizen's data (limits and the once-only obligation),
and give the framework teeth — a proportionate sanctions ladder from warning to
fine to restricted access, suspension and escalation, because a court strikes
down a sanction that is not proportionate. Invented legal text reads
convincingly and is worthless or harmful, so each article is adapted from a
named published law, and a clause with no model behind it is flagged for a
lawyer. The articles must describe exactly the exchanges the architects build —
law and code as one blueprint.
```

### 2.5 — The Cover Note and Two-Track Regulatory Memo

**File:** `video/KP2_M2_2.5_Video_v0.6.mp4` · **Subtitles:** `audio/KP2_M2_2.5_Audio_v0.6.srt`

**Title:** Getting an interoperability decree past the Ministry of Justice — and the data-protection authority

**Description:**

```text
Transmit the package with a specific ask, and show how the decree and the
data-protection law fit together — so the decree moves instead of sitting in a
queue, and the data-protection authority backs it instead of blocking it.

GIF · Module 2 · 2.5 — The Cover Note and Two-Track Regulatory Memo

A finished package with no instructions goes to the bottom of the pile. The
Cover Note is a thirty-second read with a direct ask — perfect the articles,
confirm the authorities, advise on the route to enactment, executive decree or
primary legislation — and the cost of each week of delay. Then the real
minefield: both laws govern the same data. The Two-Track Regulatory Memo maps
where they meet — definitions, consent, cross-border transfer, data-protection
officers, sanctions — and for each point says where the decree defers, where it
fills a gap, and how a conflict is resolved, turning the regulator into a
partner.
```

### 2.6 — The decree as configuration

**File:** `video/KP2_M2_2.6_Video_v0.6.mp4` · **Subtitles:** `audio/KP2_M2_2.6_Audio_v0.6.srt`

**Title:** A decree is finished when it authorises exactly what your framework carries — here's the check

**Description:**

```text
The finished decree authorises exactly the exchanges in your Use-Case Catalogue.
Read it as the legal configuration that binds the technical bus.

GIF · Module 2 · 2.6 — The decree as configuration

Law and software are two views of one system, so before anything is built, read
the decree back against the catalogue. Two checks. Coverage — no less: every
exchange in the catalogue has a lawful basis in the decree. Scope discipline —
no more: no article authorises sharing the catalogue does not need, however
tempting a broad umbrella power looks. Then two articles bind the bus: mandatory
connection makes the named agencies members, and the once-only obligation makes
a covered exchange a duty, not a favour. Once both checks pass, the legal layer
is a verified configuration.
```
