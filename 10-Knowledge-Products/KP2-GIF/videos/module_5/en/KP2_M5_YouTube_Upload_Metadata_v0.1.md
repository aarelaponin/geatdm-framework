```text
You do not onboard a whole government at once. Foundation, Pilot, Expansion,
Optimisation — four phases with decision gates, an honest calendar, and the four
plans beside the schedule that a funder actually reads.

GIF · Module 5 · 5.1 — Plan the build in four phases

The foundation — decree, governance, architecture, standards — comes first, a
```

**Title:** Connected isn't dependable: the SLA that makes a government data service reliable

**Description:**

```text
A member being connected is not the same as a member being dependable. A
Service-Level Agreement turns "connected" into "dependable" — and a template makes
it a fill-in, not a negotiation from scratch.

GIF · Module 5 · 5.3 — Make 'connected' mean 'dependable' — the SLA

Five numbers a consumer can rely on: availability, response time, support hours,
incident response, and change notice. The SLA makes the governance module's member
```

### 5.7 — From demonstration to production

**File:** `video/KP2_M5_5.7_Video_v0.9.mp4` · **Subtitles:** `audio/KP2_M5_5.7_Audio_v0.9.srt`

**Title:** What changes from an interoperability demo to a production-grade federation

**Description:**

```text
The demonstration proves the pattern. It is not production. What changes between
the sandboxed Linkup demonstration and a production-grade federation a country
would actually run — including retiring the legacy point-to-point links.

GIF · Module 5 · 5.7 — From demonstration to production

Production adds seven things around the same configuration: separate sized hosts,
a real certification authority, high availability, monitoring and alerting,
capacity for real volumes, round-the-clock support, and security hardening and
audit. The shape of the configuration does not change, which is why the
demonstration de-risks the build. Then the task the hardening list misses: migrate
each agency off its old point-to-point links — parallel-run, cut over, retire. The
demonstration names its own gap, clause by clause. Never ship it as production.

Sources
```

**Description:**

```text
A running bus is not finished — it is operating, every day. Point an AI assistant
```

```text
Keep the decree, the Governance Pack and the standards portfolio saying the same

GIF · Module 5 · 5.9 — Keep the documents honest — the consistency cross-check
```
