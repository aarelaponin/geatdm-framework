## Example input

Country: Progressa. The two brackets:

**(A) Use-Case Catalogue (B5).** 1. PNIA → PNEA: identity. 2. PLR → PNEA: enrolment. 3. PNEA → MoEYS: results. 4. Civil Registration → schools: birth facts. 5. PLR → Social Protection Agency: enrolment. 6. Social register → MoEYS: eligibility. First wave: rows 1–3.

**(B) Draft articles (B11 and the articles drafted after it).**

- **Art 3, Mandatory connection.** Every public body that holds a base registry shall connect to Linkup and publish its data services.
- **Art 5, Once-only.** At a person's request, or where [confirm: statutory provision], a designated body (Annex I: PNIA, PLR, PNEA, MoEYS) shall obtain evidence of identity, enrolment or examination results directly from the designated body that holds it.
- **Art 6, Data protection.** Each exchange is limited to the fields in Annex II. Annex II: identity — NIN, given name, family name, date of birth, sex, region; enrolment — NIN, school, level, enrolment year, status.

**Not covered.** Rows 4–6: neither provider nor consumer is in Annex I. Add them to the annex when their wave starts.

**Over-reaching**

- **Art 5.** Any designated body may fetch any of the three kinds of evidence from any other: PLR or MoEYS could fetch identity from PNIA, which no catalogue row asks for. Fix: replace the body list with an annex of exchange pairs — provider, consumer, data, purpose.
- **Art 3.** "Every public body that holds a base registry" binds bodies no catalogue row names. Fix: name the bodies in an annex and extend it by amendment.

## Reading the output

### 1. The Art 5 finding matches the running bus

The build pack's identity service grants access to one subscriber only, PNEA's EXAMS subsystem; PLR is deliberately left out. Its acceptance document asserts the list holds exactly that one entry. A decree that lets PLR fetch identity would authorise what the bus is built to refuse. The law should be at least as narrow as the configuration.

### 2. "Covered" is not "lawful"

Row 2 rests on a registry with no founding instrument. The build pack's own lawful-basis line for the enrolment service names a learner registry act and marks the wording illustrative. This check found an article; it did not find that the article can stand. That question stays with counsel, as the safeguard says.

### 3. Check fields, not only articles

Row 3 is covered by Art 5 but has no Annex II field list, so nothing limits what results PNEA sends. The run tested which articles apply, not what data each allows. Add a results field list before row 3 enters the first wave.

## What next

Fix Art 5 and Art 3, add the results field list, and run the check again before filing. The coverage table then feeds the Member Requirements checklist in [5.2](../module-5/5-2.md): a member is admitted only for exchanges the decree covers. In your framework workbook this is artefact **B13**.
