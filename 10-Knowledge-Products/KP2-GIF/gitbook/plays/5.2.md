## Reading the output

### 1. Lawful basis per service, not per member

The model stated PLR's lawful basis on its service, not on the agency as a whole. The build pack does the same: PLR's requirements file points item 5 at each service's own lawful basis. An agency that later provides a second service needs a second basis, and this shape makes that visible.

### 2. The evidence column names documents nobody holds

"A data-quality sample report" and "registration request in the queue" are what evidence should be, not what exists. The pack's `onboarding/plr/02-requirements.md` records each item as *stated by the applicant*, with no evidence attached. Item 4 is the riskiest tick: in Progressa, PLR is planned and not started, so "data cleaned" is a claim about data that has no history yet.

### 3. The decree article does not exist yet

The prompt asks for a decree article, and Progressa has none. The pack labels its "Learner Registry Act" wording as illustrative. Beside the checklist, the pack also lists two things it does not hold: the signed membership agreement and the named data-protection officer. Check both before go-live, along with the incident channel: the prompt asks for one, and the pack's checklist records only the contact.
