## Example input

Country: Progressa. The integration map (the bracket), from A0 §9:

1. PNIA → PNEA: person identity, for examination registration and certificates. Today: paper ID or birth certificate.
2. PLR → PNEA: enrolment, for the same. Today: school letter. PLR planned, not started.
3. Civil Registration → schools (MoEYS): birth facts, for primary enrolment. Today: paper birth certificate.
4. PLR → Social Protection Agency: enrolment, for the child social grant. Today: school letter.
5. Social register → MoEYS: household eligibility, for scholarships. Today: paper income proof; register held by a 2016 vendor system.
6. PNEA → MoEYS: examination results, for scholarships. Today: paper certificate.

Pasted with it, **B3:** first exchange PNEA certificate issuance ← PNIA + PLR; PNIA leg first, for candidates aged 16 and over.

**First wave**

1. **PNIA → PNEA: identity.** The provider is on Linkup with a documented API; the quickest proof of once-only.
2. **PLR → PNEA: enrolment.** Completes B3's exchange; build PLR as a Linkup provider from day one.
3. **PNEA → MoEYS: results.** PNEA will already be on Linkup after wave one; it reuses that work for the scholarship platform.

## Reading the output

### 1. The ranking follows the input

The first pick is the only exchange whose provider is already on the bus, and the shortlist reuses PNEA's onboarding twice. Ranking burden against effort, not burden alone, is what keeps the social grant row out of wave one despite its High burden.

### 2. "Low effort" on the PNIA row is optimistic

PNIA is ready, but PNEA is not a Linkup member, and the Organisational column is Missing. Low effort describes the wiring, not the service agreement. Call it Medium until PNIA has agreed a service level.

### 3. The Legal column is almost all "Partial" by default

The Data Protection Act exists, so the run marked every row Partial except the vendor register. That is a guess, not a reading of any law. The safeguard asks for these flags to be confirmed with counsel; here they barely discriminate.

### 4. What is missing: the under-16 gap

Row 1's Semantic "Partial" hides that PNIA holds no one under 16. The catalogue should say who the exchange serves, not just which bodies it joins.
