## Reading the output

### 1. Every change type points at a RACI row

No step here invents a new decider: each Accountable comes from B16, including the split between catalogue and access that the RACI review asked for. One form, one log, four rows. That is light enough to be used.

### 2. The urgent path is a new power

"Act first, confirm later" is sensible for a certificate compromise, but nothing in the RACI or the mandate gives the Security chair that power. Write it into the Security charter (B18) with a limit, such as 72 hours, or the first emergency becomes a dispute about authority.

### 3. The example entry's figures are placeholders

The 12-month transition and the two-year cycle follow the course's typical values, not a Progressa decision. The binding date is left open, correctly, because it depends on the decree. Do not copy the entry as a ruling.

### 4. Pilot it before you declare it

The safeguard asks for one real change first. The obvious one is the enrolment model: run a single OneRoster field change through the table, end to end, and time it. Also confirm the standards-portfolio owner is a real post with time, not one of the two junior analysts on top of other work.
