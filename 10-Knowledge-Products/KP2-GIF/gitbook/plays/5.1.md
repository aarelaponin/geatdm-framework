## Example input

Country: Progressa. The context bracket, taken from B5, B6 and A0 §2, §5:

**First exchanges (B5).** 1: PNEA issues a certificate without asking the candidate to prove identity or enrolment; PNEA calls PNIA's identity service and PLR's enrolment service over Linkup. First wave, in order: 1 PNIA → PNEA identity; 2 PLR → PNEA enrolment; 3 PNEA → MoEYS results, for the scholarship. Three more wait: PLR → Social Protection, Civil Registration → schools, social register → MoEYS.

**Onboarding order (B6).** Champions: PNIA, PNEA, PDGA. Early Adopter: MoEYS, which owns PLR. Observers: Social Protection, Civil Registration.

**Platform.** Linkup (X-Road 7.x) has run in pilot since 2025 with four members, operated by PDGA.

**Budget and funder.** No dedicated envelope. The National Learner Registry programme (World Bank, USD 6.5m over 3 years) carries the education integrations. Annual budget cycle; no multi-year ICT envelopes.

**Constraints.** PDGA coordinates but cannot bind. PLR is planned, not started. The Data Protection Act 2023 needs a legal basis to share a minor's data. The Public Procurement Act has no multi-year ICT framework.

## Reading the output

### 1. It read the pilot correctly

The input says Linkup has run since 2025, and the plan treats phase 1 as hardening an existing bus, not buying one. It also put PLR on the critical path: exchange 2 needs a registry that has not started, so phase 1 starts PLR's data work in month 0. Both follow from the input.

### 2. The member counts are the prompt's, not Progressa's

Fifteen members by month 18 came from the prompt, not from B6, which names six bodies in all. The plan should either name the nine more members or lower the target. Phase 4's Health and Agriculture are the prompt's next sectors too; B6 does not name them. The "state recurrent line for PDGA" is also invented: Progressa has annual budgets and no multi-year ICT envelopes, so that line is something to ask for, not a fact.

### 3. Month 0 hides a year or more

The prompt assumes the decree, governance and standards are in place at month 0. In Progressa the Roadmap is still a draft and there is no decree. Add the groundwork as phase 0 before a funder reads the calendar. Also check what happens to Linkup's four current pilot members, and to the 2022 tax link that was never migrated. Neither appears in the plan.
