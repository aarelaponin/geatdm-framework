## Example input

Country: Progressa. The exchange (the bracket):

When a learner applies to the Progressa National Examination Authority (PNEA) for a certificate, PNEA needs the learner's identity from the Progressa National ID Authority (PNIA) and proof of enrolment from the Progressa Learner Registry (PLR). Today the learner brings both on paper. The purpose is to issue the certificate without asking the learner for either.

Context pasted with it, from A0 §1 and §9:

- Linkup (X-Road 7.x, PDGA) has been in pilot since 2025. PNIA is a member; PNEA and MoEYS are not.
- PLR is planned under the Education Sector Plan 2023–2028, not started. Today learner records sit in district EMIS files with their own numbering; PNEA keeps its own candidate numbering.
- PNIA issues IDs only at 16. Birth registration is at 71%.
- The Data Protection Act 2023 requires a legal basis to share a minor's data, and parental consent for non-statutory uses.
- No data-standards function exists in the education sector.

## Example output

| Layer | Status | What is missing | The one action that closes it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Technical | Partial | Linkup exists and PNIA is on it; PNEA is not a member, and PLR has no system to connect | Onboard PNEA to Linkup; build PLR as a Linkup provider from day one |
| Semantic | Missing | Three learner numberings; no shared identifier below 16; no agreed field list for "enrolment" | Name one learner identifier and one enrolment record, owned by a named body |
| Organisational | Missing | No agreement between PNEA, PNIA and the PLR owner; no service level; PDGA coordinates but cannot bind | A signed service agreement per provider, under a body with authority to govern it |
| Legal | Partial | The Act requires a legal basis for minors' data; none names this exchange | A provision giving PNEA a statutory basis to fetch identity and enrolment |

**Binding constraint:** Semantic. Even with every body on Linkup, PNEA could not match a candidate to a PLR record, because no identifier is common to all three today.

## Reading the output

### 1. Not stopping at the technical row is earned

The input mentions Linkup first, and a weaker run would have called the exchange nearly ready. The table puts two of four layers at Missing, each tied to a fact in the input: three numberings, a coordinating mandate that does not bind.

### 2. The binding-constraint call hides a bigger problem

One of this exchange's two providers does not exist. Calling it a semantic problem is defensible, but a minister would hear "fix a code list". The honest call is that the PLR leg cannot run at all yet. The PNIA leg can, but only for learners aged 16 and over.

### 3. Legal "Partial" is only what the input said

The run had a law and an obligation to read, so it chose Partial. Nobody has checked whether PNEA's own statute already allows identity checks. Take this row to counsel before it is repeated anywhere.

### 4. What to check next: under-16 identity

If PNIA issues no ID below 16, primary-leaving candidates have no national identifier at all. Ask whether the birth registration number could serve for them, and who would own the link between the two.
