## Example input

Country: Progressa. The agencies (the bracket), from A0 §4 and §6, with the attitudes as the Strategist reads them today:

- **PNIA:** holds the National ID register; API documented, no sector adoption yet. Leadership keen to see sectors use e-KYC.
- **PNEA:** needs identity and enrolment for certificates. Leadership keen; tired of paper checks at exam time.
- **MoEYS:** owns the planned PLR (enrolment) and district EMIS. ICT Director cautious: head teachers are unionised and resisted the last EMIS change.
- **Social Protection Agency:** would consume enrolment for the child grant. Cautious: its register is tied to a 2016 vendor contract.
- **Civil Registration Department:** holds birth records, paper-first. Unknown; never engaged.
- **PDGA:** operates Linkup. Keen, but its mandate coordinates rather than binds.
- **Data Protection Commission:** six staff, no enforcement yet. Cautious about minors' data.

## Reading the output

### 1. The pairing is earned

Two Champions on one exchange, with the provider already on Linkup, is the textbook first pairing the concept describes. The input supports both attitudes, and the sequence follows from them.

### 2. The attitudes are the Strategist's guesses, and so is the tiering

Nothing in the Progressa pack records any leadership's attitude; the example input supplied them. The safeguard is right that these are a snapshot. MoEYS in particular could be a Champion tomorrow, because the single learner record is its own minister's promise.

### 3. The Data Protection Commission went missing

It was in the input and appears in no table. It is neither provider nor consumer, but it can stop the exchange on minors' data. Add it as a consulted party, engaged before the PLR leg is designed, not after.

### 4. The letter is a template, not a commitment

The letter asks each Champion for a focal point and a service level, but leaves the benefit as a bracket. For PNIA that is sector adoption; for PNEA, no paper checks at exam time; for PDGA, a first live exchange that shows Linkup can bind. Fill the bracket for each agency before it goes out, or it will read as PDGA asking for a favour.
