## Reading the output

### 1. The minors row is earned

The one obligation the input states — a legal basis for a minor's data — became the memo's central row. The run made the decree the statutory basis, so no consent is needed for these exchanges. That matches how the build pack treats the education exchanges.

### 2. The Act's content is mostly imagined

Definitions, rights, Data Protection Officers and sanctions are all marked [confirm] because the input says nothing about them. The table's shape is right; its middle column is a guess. The safeguard is plain: a memo that coordinates with a law as the model imagines it is worse than none.

### 3. Joint enforcement with a body that has never enforced

The Commission has six staff and no enforcement action yet. Putting it in the lead on breaches may be correct in law and empty in practice. Walk the memo through with the Commission before filing, and ask what it can actually take on.
