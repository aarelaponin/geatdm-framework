## Example input

**[country X]:** Progressa. **[paste the three]:**

**(A) Decree, from the operative article draft (B11).** Art. 4: "PNIA shall make person identity available over Linkup to PNEA, PLR and MoEYS for enrolment and credential issuance." Art. 6: "The Operating Authority (PDGA) shall admit members." Art. 7: "Members shall apply the approved standards of the e-Government Interoperability Framework 2021."

**(B) Governance Pack, from the change-control process and standards register (B19).** Admit a member: Responsible, the Operating Authority; Accountable, the Steering Committee, by minuted decision. The standards register is kept by the Operating Authority's standards officer. "Member: a public body with a subsystem registered on Linkup."

**(C) Standards portfolio (B22).** X-Road 7.7.0; OpenAPI 3.0.3; TLS for transport; CEDS for person data; OneRoster for enrolment; W3C Verifiable Credentials 2.0 (planned). Services catalogued: `identity-api` (access: PNEA:EXAMS only); `enrolment-api` (access: PNEA:EXAMS). "Member: an organisation that operates a security server."

## Reading the output

### 1. Finding 1 is real, and the run did not settle it

The build pack's own gate register puts the admission decision with the Steering Committee, by minuted decision, and the Governance Pack agrees. So the decree looks like the odd one out. Still, the run only asked the question, which is right: a decree can delegate to the Steering Committee, or the Governance Pack can be wrong.

### 2. Finding 3 could lead someone to the wrong fix

In the build pack, `identity-api` is granted to PNEA alone on purpose. PLR's refusal is one of the acceptance checks. A reader who takes finding 3 as a missing grant and adds PLR to the access list would undo a data-protection decision. The question is fair, but the answer is most likely "narrow the decree", and the play cannot tell you that.

### 3. A fourth document would catch more

The check saw short extracts, so a clean row means nothing was found in what was pasted. Add the gate register (one per member in the build pack). It shows, for example, that no member names a data-protection officer yet, while a decree's data-protection article may require one. That drift falls between the three documents, and no row here can see it.
