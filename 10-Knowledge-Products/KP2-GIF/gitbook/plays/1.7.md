## Example input

Sector: education. Country: Progressa.

Context pasted with it, from A0 §1: Progressa joined GovStack in 2024 and is a 50-in-5 DPI pilot country. Linkup (X-Road 7.x) is in pilot. The National ID (2018) is issued from age 16; e-KYC since 2024. MoEYS keeps district EMIS records with its own learner numbering; a National Learner Registry is planned. A 2021 e-Government Interoperability Framework names message formats and an approved-standards list but is not applied.

## Reading the output

### 1. The overlaps are the useful part

The prompt asked for competing standards to be flagged, and two real choices surface: OneRoster against CEDS for enrolment, and Open Badges against Europass for credentials. These are the decisions your standards portfolio will have to record. The adopt-first list matches what the build pack uses: OneRoster for enrolment, CEDS for person and award fields, W3C VC 2.0 as the certificate target.

### 2. Maturity labels are soft

"Growing" and "mature, mainly US" are the model's impressions, not measured adoption. CEDS is a US vocabulary; calling it international would overstate it. Check each version and status on the maintainer's own site before the table goes into a decision.

### 3. What is missing: the national profile and the identifier

No international standard gives Progressa a learner identifier, and PNIA's ID starts at 16. The national profile on top of OneRoster, naming which identifier and which local fields, is the work the run did not mention. So is the 2021 framework's approved-standards list, which should be checked for anything already adopted.

### 4. Licensing needs checking

1EdTech specifications are free to read, but conformance certification has its own terms. Confirm with the data owners that each candidate is acceptable before committing.
