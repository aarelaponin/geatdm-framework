# Task 09: Capacity Building and Risk Management

## Purpose
Write Sections 7 and 8 of the D7 EA Implementation Roadmap — Capacity Building Roadmap and Risk Management for MoH digital health transformation.

## Scope
- Section 7.1: Current Capacity Assessment (workforce baseline, competency gaps, training infrastructure)
- Section 7.2: Capacity Building Strategy (short/medium/long-term across 3 waves)
- Section 7.3: Training Plan by System (DHIS2, HIE/OpenHIM, MPI/OpenCR, EHR/OpenMRS, LIS, data governance, info security)
- Section 8.1: Risk Register (12 risks with full specification: ID, description, category, likelihood, impact, rating, mitigation, owner, contingency, monitoring indicator)
- Section 8.2: Risk Response Strategy (response classification, monitoring cadence, escalation triggers, review cycle)
- Section 8.3: Summary

## Outputs
- `D7-S09-capacity-risk.md` — Sections 7 and 8 complete content

## Key Design Decisions
1. Competency assessment uses 5-level implementation maturity model from benchmarks; MoH is Level 0–1 in 8/10 domains
2. Training plan specifies benchmarked hours per system: 20–40 hrs/clinician for EHR, 16–24 hrs for DHIS2 Tracker, 40 hrs ToT
3. Clinical informatics champion model (1:10–15 ratio) adopted from KenyaEMR/UgandaEMR experience
4. MSc Health Informatics sponsorship at KNUST/UGhana for DHDD leadership pipeline
5. Total training investment USD 590K–1.01M = 8–12% of technology investment (benchmark: 10–20%)
6. 12 risks identified: 2 Critical (EHSSP cliff, DHDD recruitment), 10 High
7. Every risk has named owner (specific body/position), not generic "project team"
8. Escalation triggers defined per risk with clear governance chain
9. UoP partnership for long-term in-country health informatics programme
