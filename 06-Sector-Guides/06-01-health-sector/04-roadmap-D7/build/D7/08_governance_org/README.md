# Task 08: Governance and Organisational Structure

## Purpose
Write Section 6 of the D7 EA Implementation Roadmap — Organisational Structure and Governance for MoH digital health transformation.

## Scope
- Section 6.1: AS-IS organisational structure (fragmented digital health teams)
- Section 6.2: TO-BE organisational structure (NDHSC, DHDD, TWGs, EA Focal Point)
- Section 6.3: Federated custodianship model (system ownership matrix, national-sector governance linkage)
- Section 6.4: Staffing plan (national, regional, facility levels with phased recruitment)
- Section 6.5: Policy support requirements (5 enabling policies)
- Section 6.6: Summary (AS-IS to TO-BE contrast)

## Outputs
- `D7-S08-governance-org.md` — Section 6 complete content

## Key Design Decisions
1. NDHSC established by Ministerial Order (not legislation) for speed — 14 members, quarterly, authority over investments >USD 50K
2. DHDD is an upgrade of existing Directorate of Planning & Information, not a new directorate from scratch
3. Federated custodianship: MoDE/PDGA = national EA custodian; MoH/DHDD = sector custodian
4. 14 core DHDD positions phased across Wave 1 (6) and Wave 2 (8)
5. 7 regional HIS focal persons deployed Wave 2a/2b
6. 5 enabling policies with responsible bodies and timelines aligned to wave phasing
