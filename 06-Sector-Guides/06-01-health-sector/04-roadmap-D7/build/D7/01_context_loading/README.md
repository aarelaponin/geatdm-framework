## Key Findings Summary

**D6 Target Architecture (task_01_doc_d6_target_summary.md):** The D6 defines a comprehensive 2025–2033 target architecture built on OpenHIE, with 12 major design decisions covering HIE (OpenHIM), MPI (OpenCR/eCRVS NIN), EHR (OpenMRS at 30–40 facilities), EIR (SPT→DHIS2 Tracker), and sovereign cloud hosting. A three-tier governance model (NDHSC, DHDD, 3 TWGs) is specified with 14 DHDD positions at full staffing.

**AS-IS Baseline (task_01_doc_asis_baseline.md):** The current state reveals severe fragmentation: 8 siloed systems, 1 operational integration flow (SPT→DHIS2), ~90% paper-based clinical registers, no shared patient identifier, no health data governance framework, and EA maturity of 2.19/5.0. Integration maturity is Level 1 (Ad-hoc). Key enabler: ProgressaPay demonstrates API-based integration IS achievable in Progressa.

**Funding Landscape (task_01_data_funding_landscape.md):** An estimated USD 220M+ in active/recent funding touches MoH digital health (EHSSP ~USD 100–150M, RDIP 50M, Global Fund ~USD 40M). Critical risk: EHSSP closes Aug 2025, creating a funding cliff for 4 operational systems (connectivity, eCRVS, e-NHIS, eLMIS infrastructure). RDIP infrastructure (G-Cloud, data centre, broadband) not ready until 2027+, creating a gap year. No MoH budget line exists for digital health operations.

**Implementation Benchmarks (task_01_data_benchmarks.md):** Comparable country evidence suggests: HIE establishment 18–24 months for minimal stack; EHR rollout to 30–40 facilities over 3–5 years; SPT→DHIS2 Tracker EIR transition over 3–5 years; LIS scaling 3–4 years for national coverage; IDSR paper→electronic 3–5 years. Training investment should be 10–20% of implementation budget. DHDD staffing target of 14 positions is consistent with small-country benchmarks of 10–20 national-level staff.
